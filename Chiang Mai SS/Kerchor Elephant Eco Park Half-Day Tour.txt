Attraction Name: Kerchor Elephant Eco Park Half-Day Tour (SIC Transfer)

Price PP: On Request

Description:
Experience an ethical and unforgettable encounter with rescued elephants at Kerchor Elephant Eco Park near Chiang Mai. Begin with a safety briefing and learn about elephant conservation before participating in a hands-on herbal and vitamin ball workshop. Feed the elephants, walk alongside them through their natural habitat, and capture memorable photographs. Complete your experience by joining the elephants for a refreshing bath and playful mud spa. End the tour with delicious Thai local cuisine before returning to Chiang Mai. This eco-friendly experience is ideal for families, couples, and wildlife enthusiasts seeking a responsible elephant encounter.
