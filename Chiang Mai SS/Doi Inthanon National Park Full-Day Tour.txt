Attraction Name: Doi Inthanon National Park Full-Day Tour (SIC Transfer)

Price PP: On Request 

Description:
Explore the natural beauty of Doi Inthanon National Park, home to Thailand's highest peak at 2,565 meters above sea level. This full-day guided tour takes you through lush mountain landscapes, scenic viewpoints, and the tranquil Ka Nature Trail before visiting the iconic Twin Royal Pagodas. Continue your journey with a visit to a local hill tribe market at Mae Klang and admire the breathtaking Wachirathan Waterfall. Perfect for nature lovers and adventure seekers, this tour offers a memorable experience of Chiang Mai's stunning highlands.

Places Covered:

* Doi Inthanon National Park
* Thailand's Highest Point
* Ka Nature Trail
* Twin Royal Pagodas (Napamathanidol & Napaphonphumisiri)
* Mae Klang Hill Tribe Market
* Wachirathan Waterfall
