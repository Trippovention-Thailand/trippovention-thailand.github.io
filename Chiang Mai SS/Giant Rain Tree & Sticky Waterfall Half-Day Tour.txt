Attraction Name: Giant Rain Tree & Sticky Waterfall Half-Day Tour (SIC Transfer)

Price PP: On Request

Description:
Discover two of Chiang Mai's unique natural attractions on this half-day guided tour. Begin with a visit to the iconic Giant Rain Tree, a magnificent centuries-old tree standing gracefully in the middle of a picturesque landscape. Continue to the famous Sticky Waterfall (Bua Tong Waterfall), where the mineral-rich limestone rocks allow you to safely climb up the cascading waterfall for a fun and refreshing experience. Enjoy scenic countryside views, a peaceful riverboat ride, and a relaxing stop at The Garden's House before returning to Chiang Mai. This tour is perfect for nature lovers, families, and adventure seekers.

Places Covered:

* Giant Rain Tree
Sticky Waterfall (Bua Tong Waterfall)
The Garden's House
