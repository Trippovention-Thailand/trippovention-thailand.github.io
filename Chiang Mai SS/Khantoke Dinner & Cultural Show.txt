Attraction Name: Khantoke Dinner & Cultural Show

Price PP: On Request

Description:
Immerse yourself in the rich traditions of Northern Thailand with an authentic Khantoke Dinner and Cultural Show. Enjoy a delicious traditional Lanna-style dinner served on a classic Khantoke tray while watching captivating live performances featuring traditional Thai and Lanna dances accompanied by folk music. Savor locally sourced organic vegetables, refillable herbal drinks, and warm Northern Thai hospitality in a beautifully decorated cultural setting. With vegetarian and Halal meal options available, this evening offers an unforgettable cultural and culinary experience for visitors of all ages.

Places Covered:

* Khantoke Dinner Hall
* Traditional Lanna Cultural Performance Stage
