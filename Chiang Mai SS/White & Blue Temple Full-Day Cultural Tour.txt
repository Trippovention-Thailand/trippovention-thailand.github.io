Attraction Name: White & Blue Temple Full-Day Cultural Tour (SIC Transfer)

Price PP: On Request

Description:
Discover the cultural treasures of Chiang Rai on this full-day guided tour featuring Thailand's most iconic contemporary temples. Visit the magnificent White Temple (Wat Rong Khun), admire the striking architecture of the Blue Temple (Wat Rong Suea Ten), and unwind at the picturesque Lalitta Café. En route, stop at the Mae Kachan Hot Springs to relax and enjoy a short break. You'll also have the option to visit the Hill Tribe & Long Neck Village (entrance fee payable separately). With comfortable transfers, a delicious lunch, and an experienced guide, this tour offers the perfect blend of culture, history, and scenic beauty.

Places Covered:

* Mae Kachan Hot Springs
* Mae Kachan Hill Tribe & Long Neck Village (Optional Visit)
* Wat Rong Khun (White Temple)
* Wat Rong Suea Ten (Blue Temple)
* Lalitta Café
