Attraction Name: Koh Samui City Tour (SIC Transfer)

Price:On Request

Description:
Discover the best of Koh Samui on a comprehensive half-day city tour that takes you to the island's most iconic landmarks, cultural attractions, and scenic viewpoints. Visit the majestic Big Buddha Temple, admire the beautiful architecture of Wat Plai Laem, and marvel at the unique Hin Ta & Hin Yai (Grandfather & Grandmother) Rocks. Stop at panoramic viewpoints offering breathtaking coastal vistas and explore local markets or souvenir shops to experience the island's vibrant culture. This tour is perfect for first-time visitors, families, couples, and anyone looking to experience the highlights of Koh Samui in a single trip.

Places Covered:

* Big Buddha Temple (Wat Phra Yai)
* Wat Plai Laem
* Chaweng Viewpoint
* Hin Ta & Hin Yai Rocks
* Na Muang Waterfall (Viewpoint)
* Local Souvenir Shop
