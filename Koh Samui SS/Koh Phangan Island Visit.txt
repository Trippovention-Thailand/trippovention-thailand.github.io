Attraction Name: Koh Phangan Island Visit by Ferry (Private Transfers)

Price PP: THB 1,620

Description:
Discover the laid-back charm and natural beauty of Koh Phangan on a full-day island visit from Koh Samui. Travel comfortably by ferry while enjoying scenic views of the Gulf of Thailand before exploring the island's pristine beaches, lush tropical landscapes, local markets, and cultural attractions at your own pace. Whether you wish to relax by the sea, visit charming cafés, or experience the island's peaceful atmosphere beyond its famous Full Moon Party, this excursion offers the perfect opportunity to discover one of Thailand's most beautiful islands.

* Koh Phangan
* Thong Sala Pier
* Selected Beaches & Local Areas (Free Time)
