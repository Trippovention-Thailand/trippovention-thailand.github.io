Attraction Name: Ang Thong Marine Park Tour by Speedboat (SIC Transfer)

Price: THB 2,200

Description:
Embark on an unforgettable full-day adventure to Ang Thong National Marine Park, a breathtaking archipelago of 42 limestone islands surrounded by the emerald waters of the Gulf of Thailand. Travel by speedboat to explore pristine beaches, hidden lagoons, and dramatic cliffs while enjoying snorkeling in crystal-clear waters teeming with marine life. Paddle through tranquil bays by kayak, soak in spectacular island scenery, and relax on untouched beaches. A delicious Thai lunch is served during the tour, making this excursion perfect for nature lovers, adventure seekers, couples, and families looking to experience one of Thailand's most stunning marine parks.

Places Covered:

* Ang Thong National Marine Park
* Koh Wua Talap
* Emerald Lake (Talay Nai)
* Mae Koh Island
* Snorkeling Site
* Kayaking Area
