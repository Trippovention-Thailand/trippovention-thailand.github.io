Attraction Name: Koh Madsum & Koh Tan Half-Day Tour (SIC Transfer)

Price: THB 2,100

Description:
Escape to two of Koh Samui's hidden island gems on this half-day speedboat adventure to Koh Madsum (Pig Island) and Koh Tan (Coral Island). Relax on the white sandy beaches of Koh Madsum, famous for its friendly free-roaming pigs and crystal-clear waters, before heading to Koh Tan, a tranquil island renowned for its vibrant coral reefs and excellent snorkeling opportunities. Enjoy kayaking through calm turquoise waters, swim in the clear blue sea, and take in the breathtaking tropical scenery. This tour is perfect for families, couples, and nature lovers looking for a fun and relaxing island escape.

Places Covered:

* Koh Madsum (Pig Island)
* Koh Tan (Coral Island)
