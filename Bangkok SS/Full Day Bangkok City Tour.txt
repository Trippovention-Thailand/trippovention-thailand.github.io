Attraction Name: Full Day Bangkok City Tour

Price: THB 1,890 (1 -3 Pax)

Description:
Discover the best of Bangkok on a full-day city tour covering the capital's most iconic cultural and historical landmarks. Visit magnificent temples, admire stunning Thai architecture, explore bustling local markets, and experience the city's unique blend of tradition and modernity. Depending on the selected itinerary, popular attractions may include the Grand Palace, Wat Phra Kaew (Temple of the Emerald Buddha), Wat Pho (Temple of the Reclining Buddha), Wat Arun (Temple of Dawn), Chinatown, Flower Market, and other famous city highlights. This tour is perfect for first-time visitors looking to explore Bangkok's rich heritage, vibrant streets, and must-see attractions in a single day.

* Wat Pho (Temple of the Reclining Buddha)
* Wat Arun (Temple of Dawn)
