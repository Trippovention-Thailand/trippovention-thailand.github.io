Attraction Name: Mahanakhon SkyWalk

Price PP: THB 2,016

Description: Visit the iconic King Power Mahanakhon SkyWalk and enjoy breathtaking panoramic views of Bangkok from one of Thailand's tallest buildings. Experience the thrilling Glass Tray on the 78th floor, offering a unique perspective of the city below. Admire spectacular daytime views of Bangkok's skyline, the Chao Phraya River, and famous city landmarks from the observation decks. A perfect experience for photography enthusiasts and anyone looking to witness Bangkok from above.

Inclusions:

* Admission ticket to King Power Mahanakhon SkyWalk
* Access to the Indoor Observation Deck (74th Floor)
* Access to the Rooftop Observation Deck & Glass Tray (78th Floor)
* Private hotel pick-up and drop-off by air-conditioned vehicle

* Any additional activities not mentioned in the inclusions
