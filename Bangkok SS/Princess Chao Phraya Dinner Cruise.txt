Attraction Name: Princess Chao Phraya Dinner Cruise

Price PP: Thb 1920

Description: Enjoy a luxurious evening aboard the Chao Phraya River with the Princess Dinner Cruise, offering stunning views of Bangkok’s illuminated landmarks. Sail past iconic sites like Wat Arun and the Grand Palace while enjoying a delicious international buffet dinner. Experience live music and entertainment in a romantic and relaxing ambiance. This cruise is a perfect way to explore Bangkok’s nightlife with scenic river views.

* International buffet dinner (veg & non-veg options)
* English / Thai Speaking staff.
* Live music & entertainment.

Exclusions:

* Personal expenses
* Tips & gratuities
* Beverages (if not included)

Timing: 18:00 TO 21:30
