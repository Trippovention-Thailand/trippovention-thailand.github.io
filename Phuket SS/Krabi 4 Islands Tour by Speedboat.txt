Attraction Name: Krabi 4 Islands Tour by Speedboat

Price: THB 930 per person

Description: Explore the famous 4 islands of Krabi on a speedboat tour covering Poda Island, Chicken Island, Tub Island, and Phra Nang Cave Beach. Walk along the unique sandbank connecting islands, relax on white sandy beaches, and enjoy snorkeling in clear turquoise waters. Capture stunning views of limestone cliffs and tropical scenery. A perfect island-hopping experience in Krabi.

Inclusions:

* Speedboat transfers
* Lunch box
* Hotel transfers
* Snorkeling equipment
* Drinking water
* English-speaking guide

Exclusions:

* National park fee (THB 200 Adult / THB 100 Child)
* Personal expenses
* Tips & gratuities
* Additional water activities

Meet-up Location: Hotel lobby (pickup from Krabi hotel)

Timing: 08:30 AM – 03:30 PM (approx.)

Duration of Tour: Full Day (Approx. 6–7 Hours)
