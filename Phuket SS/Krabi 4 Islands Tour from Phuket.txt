Attraction Name: Krabi 4 Islands Tour from Phuket

Price: THB 1,620 per person

Description: Enjoy a full-day island adventure from Phuket to the beautiful islands of Krabi. Visit popular spots including Poda Island, Chicken Island, Tub Island, and Phra Nang Cave Beach. Travel by a combination of big boat and speedboat, enjoy snorkeling, beach time, and scenic limestone cliffs. A perfect day trip to explore Krabi’s famous islands from Phuket.

Inclusions:

* Big boat & speedboat transfers
* Lunch
* Hotel transfers
* Snorkeling equipment
* Drinking water
* English-speaking guide

Exclusions:

* National park fee (THB 200 Adult / THB 100 Child)
* Personal expenses
* Tips & gratuities
* Additional water activities

Meet-up Location: Hotel lobby (pickup from Phuket hotel)

Timing: 07:00 AM – 06:00 PM

Duration of Tour: Full Day (Approx. 10–11 Hours)
