Attraction Name: 7 Islands Tour by Speedboat 

Price: THB 1,300 per person

Description: Enjoy an exciting island-hopping experience in Krabi with a speedboat tour covering stunning islands like Poda Island, Chicken Island, Tub Island, and Phra Nang Cave Beach. Swim, snorkel, and relax on beautiful beaches before enjoying a breathtaking sunset over the Andaman Sea. End the tour with a delicious beachside dinner and optional night snorkeling to witness glowing plankton. A perfect mix of adventure and scenic beauty.

Inclusions:

* Speedboat transfers
* Sunset dinner
* Hotel transfers
* Snorkeling equipment
* Drinking water
* English-speaking guide

Exclusions:

* National park fee (THB 200 Adult / THB 100 Child)
* Personal expenses
* Tips & gratuities
* Additional water activities

Meet-up Location: Hotel lobby (pickup from Krabi hotel)

Timing: 02:00 PM – 08:00 PM (approx.)

Duration of Tour: Approx. 6 Hours
