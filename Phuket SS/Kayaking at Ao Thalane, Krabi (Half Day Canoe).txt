Attraction Name: Kayaking at Ao Thalane, Krabi (Half Day Canoe)

Price: THB 693 per person

Description: Explore the natural beauty of Ao Thalane with a half-day kayaking experience through mangrove forests, limestone cliffs, and calm lagoons. Paddle through narrow canyons and hidden caves while enjoying scenic views and wildlife. This peaceful adventure offers a perfect mix of nature and soft adventure. Ideal for nature lovers and beginners alike.

Inclusions:

* Round trip transfers
* Canoe / kayak equipment
* Life jacket & dry bag
* Drinking water
* Insurance
* English-speaking guide

Exclusions:

* Lunch (included only in full-day trip)
* Personal expenses
* Tips & gratuities

Meet-up Location: Hotel lobby (pickup from Krabi hotel)

Timing: Morning / Afternoon slots (as per availability

Duration of Tour: Half Day (Approx. 4–5 Hours)
