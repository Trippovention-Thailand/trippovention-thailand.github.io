Attraction Name: Hong Island Tour by Speedboat

Price: THB 1,270 per person

Description: Explore the stunning Hong Islands on a speedboat tour, known for its emerald waters and dramatic limestone cliffs. Visit the beautiful Hong Lagoon, swim in crystal-clear waters, and relax on pristine white sandy beaches. Enjoy snorkeling around nearby islands with vibrant marine life and scenic views. A perfect island-hopping experience combining adventure and relaxation.

Inclusions:

* Speedboat transfers
* Lunch box
* Hotel transfers
* Snorkeling equipment
* Drinking water
* English-speaking guide

Exclusions:

* National park fee (THB 300 Adult / THB 150 Child)
* Personal expenses
* Tips & gratuities
* Additional water activities

Meet-up Location: Hotel lobby (pickup from Krabi hotel)

Timing: 08:30 AM – 03:30 PM (approx.)

Duration of Tour: Full Day (Approx. 6–7 Hours)
