Attraction Name: Mai Khao Plane Spotting

1. Price:

Description: Visit Mai Khao Beach for a unique plane spotting experience where aircraft fly just above your head during landing. Located near Phuket International Airport, this spot offers thrilling views of low-flying planes combined with a scenic beach setting. Capture amazing photos as planes approach the runway while enjoying the peaceful surroundings of Mai Khao Beach. A must-visit for aviation enthusiasts and photographers.
