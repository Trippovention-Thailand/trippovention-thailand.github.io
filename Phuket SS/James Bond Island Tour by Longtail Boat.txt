Attraction Name: James Bond Island Tour by Longtail Boat (No Canoe)

Price:

Adult: THB 810
Child: THB 670

Description: Explore the famous James Bond Island in Phang Nga Bay on a traditional longtail boat tour. Visit the iconic limestone karsts, enjoy scenic views, and explore nearby caves and coastal landscapes. Experience local sightseeing around fishing villages and mangrove areas, followed by a relaxing lunch. A great option for those looking for a cultural and scenic island experience.

Inclusions:

* Longtail boat transfers
* Lunch
* Hotel transfers
* Sightseeing as per program
* Drinking water
* English-speaking guide

Exclusions:

* National park fee (THB 300 Adult / THB 150 Child)
* Canoeing
* Personal expenses
* Tips & gratuities

Meet-up Location: Hotel lobby 

Timing: 09:00 AM – 06:00 PM (approx.)

Duration of Tour: Full Day (Approx. 8–9 Hours)
