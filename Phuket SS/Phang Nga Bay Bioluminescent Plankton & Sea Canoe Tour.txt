Attraction Name: Phang Nga Bay Bioluminescent Plankton & Sea Canoe Tour

Price:

Description: Experience a magical evening in Phang Nga Bay with a unique combination of sea canoeing and bioluminescent exploration. Paddle through limestone caves, hidden lagoons, and mangrove forests during the day, followed by a relaxing evening on the water. As night falls, witness the enchanting glow of bioluminescent plankton lighting up the sea in shimmering blue-green hues. This rare natural phenomenon creates a surreal and unforgettable experience, making it one of the most unique tours in Thailand.
