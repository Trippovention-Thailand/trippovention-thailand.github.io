Attraction Name: Krabi 4 Islands Tour by Longtail Boat

Price: THB 700 per person

Description: Experience a traditional island-hopping tour in Krabi by longtail boat, visiting Poda Island, Chicken Island, Tub Island, and Phra Nang Cave Beach. Walk along the unique sandbar connecting islands, relax on scenic beaches, and enjoy snorkeling in clear waters. Capture stunning limestone cliffs and tropical landscapes. A peaceful and authentic way to explore Krabi’s islands.

Inclusions:

* Longtail boat transfers
* Lunch box
* Hotel transfers
* Snorkeling equipment
* Drinking water
* English-speaking guide

Exclusions:

* National park fee (THB 200 Adult / THB 100 Child)
* Personal expenses
* Tips & gratuities
* Additional water activities

Meet-up Location: Hotel lobby (pickup from Krabi hotel)

Timing: 08:30 AM – 03:30 PM (approx.)

Duration of Tour: Full Day (Approx. 6–7 Hours)
