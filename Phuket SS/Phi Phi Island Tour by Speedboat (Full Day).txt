Attraction Name: Phi Phi Island Tour by Speedboat (Full Day)

Price: THB 1,735 per person

Description: Explore the breathtaking beauty of Phi Phi Islands on a full-day speedboat tour covering its most iconic locations. Visit the world-famous Maya Bay, known for its crystal-clear waters and white sandy beach, followed by a stop at Pileh Lagoon where you can swim in emerald-green waters surrounded by limestone cliffs. Pass by Viking Cave and enjoy snorkeling in vibrant coral reefs with colorful marine life. Relax on pristine beaches and soak in the tropical scenery, making it a perfect island getaway experience.

Inclusions:

* Speedboat transfers
* Lunch box
* National park fee
* Snorkeling equipment
* Drinking water
* English-speaking guide

Exclusions:

* Personal expenses
* Tips & gratuities
* Additional water activities

Meet-up Location: Hotel lobby (pickup from Krabi hotel)


Timing: 11:00 AM – 07:00 PM

Duration of Tour: Full Day (Approx. 8 Hours)
