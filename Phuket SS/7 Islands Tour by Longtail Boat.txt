Attraction Name: 7 Islands Tour by Longtail Boat 

Price: THB 950 per person

Description: Experience a magical evening island-hopping tour in Krabi visiting multiple islands including Poda Island, Chicken Island, Tub Island, and Phra Nang Cave Beach. Enjoy swimming, snorkeling, and relaxing on beautiful beaches before witnessing a stunning sunset over the Andaman Sea. End the tour with a delicious beachside dinner and optional night snorkeling to see glowing plankton. A perfect blend of adventure and romantic evening experience.

Inclusions:

* Longtail boat transfers
* Sunset dinner
* Hotel transfers
* Snorkeling equipment
* Drinking water
* English-speaking guide

Exclusions:

* National park fee (THB 200 Adult / THB 100 Child)
* Personal expenses
* Tips & gratuities
* Additional water activities

Meet-up Location: Hotel lobby (pickup from Krabi hotel)

Timing: 02:00 PM – 08:00 PM (approx.)

Duration of Tour:Approx. 6 Hours
