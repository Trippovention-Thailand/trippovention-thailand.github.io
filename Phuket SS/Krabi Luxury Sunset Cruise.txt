Attraction Name: Krabi Luxury Sunset Cruise

Price:


Description: Enjoy a relaxing evening cruise in Krabi with stunning views of the Andaman Sea. Sail past beautiful limestone cliffs and islands while enjoying snorkeling in clear waters. As the sun sets, take in breathtaking views and unwind on board in a peaceful atmosphere. End the experience with a delicious dinner while cruising back under the evening sky. A perfect blend of relaxation, scenery, and light adventure.

* Insurance

* Additional beverages (if any)
