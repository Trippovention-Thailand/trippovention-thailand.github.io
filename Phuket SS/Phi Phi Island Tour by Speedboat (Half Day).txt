Attraction Name: Phi Phi Island Tour by Speedboat (Half Day)

Price: THB 1,275 per person

Description: Discover the beauty of Phi Phi Islands on a half-day speedboat tour covering its most scenic highlights. Visit the iconic Maya Bay, relax at Pileh Lagoon surrounded by limestone cliffs, and pass by Viking Cave. Enjoy swimming and snorkeling in crystal-clear waters with vibrant marine life. A perfect short island escape with stunning tropical views.
