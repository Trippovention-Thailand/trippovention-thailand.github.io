Attraction Name: Hong Island Tour by Longtail Boat

Price: THB 1,040 per person

Description: Discover the untouched beauty of Hong Islands on a longtail boat tour. Explore the stunning Hong Lagoon surrounded by limestone cliffs, relax on pristine beaches, and enjoy swimming in crystal-clear waters. Visit nearby islands and enjoy snorkeling with vibrant marine life. A peaceful and scenic island-hopping experience perfect for nature lovers.

Inclusions:

* Longtail boat transfers
* Lunch box
* Hotel transfers
* Snorkeling equipment
* Drinking water
* English-speaking guide

Exclusions:

* National park fee (THB 300 Adult / THB 150 Child)
* Personal expenses
* Tips & gratuities
* Additional water activities

Meet-up Location: Hotel lobby (pickup from Krabi hotel)

Timing: 08:30 AM – 03:30 PM (approx.)

Duration of Tour: Full Day (Approx. 6–7 Hours)
