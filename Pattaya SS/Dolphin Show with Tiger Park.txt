Attraction Name: Dolphin Show with Tiger Park 

Price: THB 2,006

Description:
Experience an unforgettable wildlife adventure in Pattaya with a visit to the Pattaya Dolphinarium and Tiger Park. Begin your journey by watching the spectacular Dolphin Show, where highly trained dolphins perform amazing acrobatic stunts, synchronized routines, and interactive performances that delight visitors of all ages. Continue to Tiger Park, where you'll have the opportunity to meet and take photos with a Medium Tiger under the supervision of trained professionals in a safe and controlled environment. This tour offers the perfect combination of entertainment and wildlife encounters, making it an ideal choice for families, couples, and animal lovers.

Places Covered:

* Pattaya Dolphinarium
* Tiger Park Pattaya (Medium Tiger Experience)
