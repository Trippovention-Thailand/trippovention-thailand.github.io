Attraction Name: Coral Island Tour (Koh Larn)

Price PP: THB 210

Description:
Spend a memorable day at Coral Island (Koh Larn), one of Pattaya's most popular tropical destinations, known for its crystal-clear turquoise waters, soft white sandy beaches, and vibrant marine life. Travel by speedboat to the island and enjoy leisure time relaxing on the beach, swimming in the sea, or soaking in the scenic coastal views. Adventure seekers can also choose from a variety of exciting water sports such as parasailing, jet skiing, banana boat rides, and sea walking (available at an additional cost). Complete your island getaway with a delicious lunch before returning to Pattaya. This tour is perfect for families, couples, friends, and beach lovers looking for a fun-filled tropical escape.
