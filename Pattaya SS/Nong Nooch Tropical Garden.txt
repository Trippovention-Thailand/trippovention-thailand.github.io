Attraction Name: Nong Nooch Tropical Garden with Sightseeing Bus & Cultural Show (Private Transfer)

Price: THB 1,596 

Description:
Visit the world-renowned Nong Nooch Tropical Garden, one of Pattaya's most popular attractions, spread across beautifully landscaped gardens featuring exotic plants, themed zones, and stunning floral displays. Explore the expansive grounds comfortably aboard the sightseeing bus, allowing you to enjoy the garden's highlights with ease. Experience the entertaining Thai Cultural Show, showcasing traditional dance, music, martial arts, and cultural performances, followed by the exciting Elephant Show featuring the intelligence and skills of these gentle giants. This attraction offers a perfect blend of nature, culture, and entertainment, making it an ideal outing for families, couples, and visitors of all ages.
