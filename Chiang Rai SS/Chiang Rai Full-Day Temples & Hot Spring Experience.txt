Attraction Name: Chiang Rai Full-Day Temples & Hot Spring Experience (SIC Transfer)

Price PP: On Request

Description:
Discover the cultural and natural highlights of Chiang Rai on this full-day guided excursion from Chiang Mai. Relax at the famous Mae Kachan Hot Springs before visiting the stunning Blue Temple (Wat Rong Suea Ten) and the world-renowned White Temple (Wat Rong Khun), celebrated for their remarkable architecture and intricate artwork. Enjoy a delicious local lunch, unwind at the picturesque Lalitta Café, and visit the peaceful Wat Huai Pha Kiang. This tour perfectly combines spirituality, scenic landscapes, and authentic Northern Thai culture, making it an ideal day trip for travelers seeking the best of Chiang Rai.

Places Covered:

* Mae Kachan Hot Springs
* Wat Rong Suea Ten (Blue Temple)
* Wat Rong Khun (White Temple)
* Lalitta Café
* Wat Huai Pha Kiang
