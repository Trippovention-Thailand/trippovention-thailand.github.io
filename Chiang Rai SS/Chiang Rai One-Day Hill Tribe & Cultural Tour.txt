Attraction Name: Chiang Rai One-Day Hill Tribe & Cultural Tour (SIC Transfer)

Price PP: On Request

Description:
Experience the authentic culture and natural beauty of Chiang Rai on this immersive full-day tour. Visit traditional Lisu, Akha, and Lahu hill tribe villages to learn about their unique customs and lifestyles. Enjoy a hands-on bamboo cooking experience where you'll prepare and savor a delicious local lunch using handmade bamboo utensils. Continue to a scenic Chinese Village, explore a lush tea plantation, admire a beautiful waterfall, and unwind at a natural hot spring. This tour is perfect for travelers seeking a blend of cultural experiences, local traditions, and Northern Thailand's picturesque countryside.

* Chinese Village

* Akha Village
* Lahu Hill Tribe Village
* Hot Spring
