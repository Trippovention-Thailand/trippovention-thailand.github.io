Description:
Discover the cultural and royal heritage of Chiang Rai on this half-day guided tour to two of the province's most iconic landmarks. Visit the sacred Wat Phra That Doi Tung, one of Northern Thailand's oldest and most revered temples, perched atop Doi Tung Mountain with panoramic views of the surrounding countryside. Continue to the elegant Doi Tung Royal Villa, the former residence of the Princess Mother, renowned for its beautiful architecture and peaceful mountain setting. This tour is ideal for travelers seeking history, culture, and scenic beauty in a single experience.
