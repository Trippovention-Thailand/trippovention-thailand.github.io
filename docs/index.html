Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about our Thailand travel packages, sightseeing, transfers, and ground services

What Thailand destinations does Trippovention cover?

Trippovention covers all major destinations across Thailand including Bangkok, Pattaya, Phuket, Krabi, Chiang Mai, Koh Samui, Ayutthaya, Hua Hin, Koh Phangan, Koh Larn, Koh Yao Yai, and Koh Mook. We arrange land packages, transfers, sightseeing, and day trips across all these regions.

Can I book only sightseeing without a holiday package?

Yes, absolutely. You can book individual sightseeing experiences, attraction tickets, day tours, dinner cruises, theme parks, and cultural activities without needing to book a complete holiday package.

Do you provide private airport transfers in Thailand?

Yes, we provide private, air-conditioned airport transfers with verified professional drivers from Bangkok Suvarnabhumi (BKK), Don Mueang (DMK), and Phuket International Airport (HKT) directly to your hotel, as well as intercity routes such as Bangkok to Pattaya or Hua Hin.

Can I combine multiple Thailand destinations?

Yes. We specialize in multi-destination Thailand combinations such as Bangkok + Pattaya, Bangkok + Phuket, Phuket + Krabi, Bangkok + Pattaya + Phuket, and Northern Thailand cultural circuits, complete with coordinated domestic transfers, ferries, and stays.

Do you offer Thailand day trips?

Yes. We offer convenient day trips from major Thailand hubs, including Ayutthaya historical tours from Bangkok, Coral Island speedboat excursions from Pattaya, Phi Phi Island trips from Phuket, and Emerald Pool nature tours from Krabi.

Can I customize a Thailand holiday?

Yes, all our Thailand land packages are fully flexible. You can customize travel dates, destinations, hotel categories, private vehicle transfers, meal preferences, and daily itineraries to fit your exact needs and budget.

Do you offer Buddha Circuit journeys?

Yes. Trippovention organizes dedicated Buddha Circuit spiritual journeys and pilgrimage tours to sacred Buddhist heritage sites in India (including Bodh Gaya, Sarnath, Kushinagar, Rajgir, Nalanda, and Varanasi), specially designed for Thai Buddhist travellers, monks, families, and pilgrimage groups with Thai-friendly hospitality and arrangements.

Can international travellers book Thailand travel with Trippovention?

Yes. We cater to international travellers and travel trade partners from India, the United Kingdom, Europe, the USA, the Middle East, and Asia-Pacific, offering English-speaking on-ground assistance, specialized dietary options (Vegetarian, Jain, Halal), and secure booking coordination.

When will online reservations be available?

Our online reservation system is currently in development and coming soon. It will allow guests and travel partners to conveniently explore and reserve Thailand packages, sightseeing, transfers, day trips, and India Buddha Circuit journeys online. In the meantime, our reservations desk confirms inquiries quickly via our contact form and WhatsApp.
