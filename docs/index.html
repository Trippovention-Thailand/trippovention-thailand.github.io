Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about our Thailand travel packages, sightseeing, transfers, and services

Which destinations in Thailand do you cover?

Trippovention covers all major destinations across Thailand including Bangkok, Pattaya, Phuket, Krabi, Chiang Mai, Koh Samui, Phi Phi Island, Koh Samet, Koh Yao Yai, Koh Mook, Hua Hin, and Ayutthaya. We arrange custom multi-city itineraries connecting these regions seamlessly.

Do you offer customized Thailand holiday packages?

Yes, all our Thailand tour packages are fully customizable. You can personalize your travel dates, preferred destinations, hotel category (3-star, 4-star, 5-star, or luxury pool villas), sightseeing tours, and pace of travel to match your budget and expectations.

What sightseeing tours and experiences can I book?

We provide private and shared (SIC) sightseeing tours including Bangkok city and temple tours, Chao Phraya dinner cruises, Safari World, Mahanakhon SkyWalk, Pattaya Sanctuary of Truth, Coral Island speedboats, Phuket island hopping, Phi Phi & Maya Bay tours, and Krabi 4 Islands excursions with licensed guides.

How do your Thailand private transfers work?

We offer private, air-conditioned airport transfers from Suvarnabhumi (BKK), Don Mueang (DMK), and Phuket (HKT) airports directly to your hotel. We also provide intercity transfers (such as Bangkok to Pattaya, Bangkok to Hua Hin, and Phuket to Krabi) with verified professional drivers and flight tracking for punctual pickups.

Can you arrange hotel accommodations for my trip?

Yes, we partner directly with certified hotels, boutique properties, and luxury beachfront resorts across Thailand. We can include standard, deluxe, family interconnecting rooms, pool villas, and daily breakfast tailored to your preferences and budget.

Do you assist with international flights and Thailand visa requirements?

Yes, our travel desk provides international flight ticketing and updated guidance on Thailand entry regulations, visa exemptions, and visa-on-arrival processes to ensure a seamless departure and arrival experience.

Can you organize private group travel and family vacations?

Absolutely. We specialize in private group travel, family holidays with children or senior citizens, and multi-generational reunions. We arrange private coaches, kid-friendly sightseeing itineraries, and special meal preferences (including vegetarian, Jain, and Halal dining options).

Do you arrange corporate travel, conferences, and M.I.C.E. events?

Yes, Trippovention provides end-to-end corporate travel services, company retreats, incentive trips, and M.I.C.E. (Meetings, Incentives, Conferences, and Exhibitions) events across Bangkok, Pattaya, and Phuket, with dedicated event coordinators, stage/AV setup, and gala dinner arrangements.
