Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about our Thailand day trips and excursions

What should I bring on an island speedboat day trip?

We recommend bringing swimwear, a towel, reef-safe sunscreen, sunglasses, a waterproof phone pouch, and a small amount of cash for national park entry fees or personal purchases.

Are national park fees included?

Certain island tours like Phi Phi Islands or Ang Thong Marine Park have government park fees payable in cash at the pier unless specified as included in your private booking.

What happens in case of bad weather?

If the Marine Department issues a weather warning preventing boat departures, you can reschedule to another day or receive a full refund.

Can day trips be booked privately?

Yes, all our day trips are available both as shared tours (SIC) or as private VIP excursions with private speedboats and vehicles.
