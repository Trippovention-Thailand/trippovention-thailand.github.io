Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about booking Thailand attractions and sightseeing

Can I book sightseeing tickets without a full tour package?

Yes, absolutely! You can purchase individual attraction tickets, shows, cruises, and experiences without booking hotel packages or flights through us.

How do I receive my attraction tickets?

Once your booking is confirmed, official e-vouchers are delivered instantly via WhatsApp and Email with QR codes ready for presentation at the entrance.

Are hotel transfers included with sightseeing?

Most tours offer both options: ticket-only (ticket with own transport) or ticket with round-trip shared (SIC) or private vehicle transfers.

Can I book last-minute tickets?

Yes, we handle same-day and next-day ticket issuance for popular shows and attractions. Message our WhatsApp desk for fast confirmation.
