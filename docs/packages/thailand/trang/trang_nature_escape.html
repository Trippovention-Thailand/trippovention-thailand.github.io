Trang Nature Escape

Experience the hidden charm of Trang with scenic countryside, cultural landmarks, and the tranquil beauty of Koh Mook—perfect for a peaceful tropical getaway.

Duration

Detailed Itinerary

Day 1

Arrival in Trang & Nature Experience

  • Arrive at Trang International Airport.
  • Meet & greet by our local representative.
  • Transfer to your hotel by private vehicle.
  • Check in and relax after your journey.
  • Visit the beautiful Cervidae Farm, where you can enjoy the peaceful surroundings and interact with friendly deer.
  • Return to the hotel for an overnight stay.
Meals: None
Tickets: Included
Day 2

Trang City Tour & Transfer to Koh Mook

  • Breakfast at the hotel.
  • Check out from the hotel.
  • Proceed for a Trang City Tour.
  • Explore Trang's cultural landmarks, local markets, and charming city attractions.
  • Transfer from Trang Hotel to Koh Mook Hotel by private vehicle and long-tail boat.
  • Arrive at Koh Mook and check in to your beachfront resort.
  • Spend the evening relaxing amidst the island's tranquil surroundings.
Meals: Breakfast
Tickets: Included
Day 3

Departure

  • Breakfast at the resort.
  • Check out from the hotel.
  • Transfer from Koh Mook to Trang International Airport by long-tail boat and private vehicle.
  • Depart with unforgettable memories of your Trang and Koh Mook holiday.
Meals: Breakfast
