Discover the natural beauty of Krabi with stunning tropical islands, crystal-clear lagoons, and breathtaking landscapes—perfect for friends seeking adventure and unforgettable memories.

Detailed Itinerary

Day 1

Arrival in Krabi

  • Arrive at Krabi International Airport.
  • Meet & greet by our local representative.
  • Transfer to your hotel by private vehicle.
  • Check in and relax after your journey.
  • Spend the remainder of the day at leisure within the hotel premises.
Day 2

Krabi Four Island Adventure

  • Breakfast at the hotel.
  • Proceed for the Krabi Four Island Tour.
  • Visit the spectacular Chicken Island, Tup Island, Poda Island, and Phra Nang Cave Beach.
  • Enjoy swimming, snorkeling, and relaxing on the pristine white-sand beaches.
  • Savor a delicious lunch during the tour.
  • Return to the hotel in the evening.
Meals: Breakfast & Lunch
Tickets: Included
Day 3

Emerald Pool & Blue Lagoon Excursion

  • Breakfast at the hotel.
  • Proceed for the Emerald Pool & Blue Lagoon Tour.
  • Visit the famous Emerald Pool, known for its naturally crystal-clear mineral waters.
  • Explore the stunning Blue Lagoon (Blue Pool) surrounded by lush rainforest.
  • Experience the tranquil beauty of Krabi's natural attractions.
  • Return to the hotel in the evening.
Meals: Breakfast
Tickets: Included
Day 4

Departure

  • Breakfast at the hotel.
  • Check out from the hotel.
  • Transfer to Krabi International Airport by private vehicle.
  • Depart with unforgettable memories of your Krabi getaway.
Meals: Breakfast
