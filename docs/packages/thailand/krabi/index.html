Frequently Asked Questions About Krabi Tours

Quick facts on island hopping, national park fees, pickup points, and best seasons

What is the difference between Krabi 4 Islands and Hong Islands?

The 4 Islands tour visits Koh Poda, Chicken Island, the famous Tup Island sandbar (Talay Waek), and Phra Nang Cave Beach. The Hong Islands tour travels northwest to Than Bok Khorani National Park, focusing on the enclosed emerald Hong Lagoon, Lading Island, and 360-degree panoramic viewpoints.

Are National Park fees included in Krabi day tours?

National Park fees for marine tours (such as 4 Islands and 7 Islands: THB 200/adult, THB 100/child) are typically collected directly in cash on-site by government park rangers. Our Hot Springs & Emerald Pool tour includes all reserve admission fees upfront.

Where do Krabi day tours pick up from?

Complimentary round-trip hotel lobby pick-up is included for all central hotels located in Ao Nang, Nopparat Thara, and Krabi Town. Guests staying in Railay Beach meet at the designated pier or beach departure point.

When is the best time of year to visit Krabi?

The ideal weather window is between November and April when seas are calm, skies are clear, and underwater visibility is at its peak for snorkeling and boat excursions.
