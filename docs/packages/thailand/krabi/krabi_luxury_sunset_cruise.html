Tour Overview & Description

Modern Sailing Catamaran, Andaman Sunset Vistas, Gourmet Thai Dinner, SUP & Snorkeling

Step aboard an elegant, modern sailing catamaran for an exquisite afternoon and evening along Krabi's majestic coastline. Designed for comfort and understated luxury, this cruise offers ample lounge seating, shaded decks, and forward trampoline netting for sunbathing.

Cruise past towering karst cliffs, swim and snorkel in crystal-clear waters, try stand-up paddleboarding (SUP), and witness the sun sink beneath the Andaman Sea in a brilliant blaze of gold and pink. As twilight falls, enjoy a freshly prepared Thai gourmet dinner including signature Pad Thai and Salt Baked Shrimp served under the evening sky.

Places Covered & Highlights

VIP Comfort

Onboard Luxury & Watersports

  • Spacious Modern Catamaran: Dual-hull stability, cushioned open-air lounges, shaded dining saloon, and expansive sun-deck trampolines.
  • Stand-Up Paddleboards (SUP): Glide smoothly across calm island coves on premium inflatable paddleboards.
  • Guided Snorkeling Stops: Explore rich coral channels around coastal islands with provided high-clarity masks.
Culinary Journey

Gourmet Dining & Sunset

  • Signature Thai Dinner: Authentic Thai delicacies prepared fresh, featuring Pad Thai, Salt Baked Shrimp, chicken curry, and salads.
  • Golden Hour Panorama: Unmatched photographic angles of Krabi's karst monoliths silhouetted against the sunset.
  • Starlit Cruise Return: Sail gently back to port with ambient lounge music under a canopy of tropical stars.

This day tour typically includes roundtrip transport, licensed English-speaking guidance, safety equipment (such as life jackets and snorkeling gear for marine tours), drinking water, and lunch where stated in the itinerary. National park entry fees may be included or paid on-site.

Complimentary shared or private hotel pickup is available from central hotels across Krabi. Your exact pickup time and driver contact will be reconfirmed in your booking voucher prior to departure.

We recommend bringing swimwear, a towel, sunscreen, sunglasses, comfortable walking or water shoes, a waterproof bag for mobile devices, and local Thai Baht cash for national park fees or personal souvenirs.
