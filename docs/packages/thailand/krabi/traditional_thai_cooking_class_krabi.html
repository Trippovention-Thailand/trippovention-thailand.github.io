Tour Overview & Description

Organic Herb Garden Tour, Hands-On Wok Cooking, Curry Paste Grinding & 5-Course Feasting

Immerse your senses in the vibrant aromas and bold flavors of Thai culinary tradition. Hosted in a beautiful open-air garden kitchen setting, this hands-on workshop unlocks the secret balance of sweet, sour, salty, and spicy that defines Thai cuisine.

Begin with a guided tour of an organic herb garden to pick and smell fresh galangal, lemongrass, sweet Thai basil, and kaffir lime leaves. Under the step-by-step guidance of a master Thai chef, pound authentic curry paste from scratch using a stone mortar and pestle, cook 5 signature dishes at your personal wok station, and savor your creations in a serene open-air dining pavilion.

Places Covered & Highlights

Experience the highlights and sights included on this journey

Hands-On

Culinary Experience

  • Organic Herb Garden Tour: Learn to identify and harvest traditional Thai medicinal herbs, roots, and aromatics.
  • Stone Mortar & Pestle Grinding: Master the age-old art of grinding fresh chilies, herbs, and spices into fragrant curry pastes.
  • Individual Cooking Station: Cook each dish at your dedicated high-flame wok station with fresh premium ingredients.
5 Classic Dishes

Menu Highlights

  • Signature Pad Thai: Wok-tossed rice noodles with tamarind sauce, tofu, peanuts, and lime.
  • Green Curry or Massaman Curry: Rich coconut milk curry cooked with your handcrafted curry paste.
  • Tom Yum Goong or Tom Kha Gai: Iconic spicy-sour soup flavored with lemongrass and galangal.
  • Mango Sticky Rice & Spring Rolls: Crispy golden appetizer and Thailand's beloved coconut mango dessert.
