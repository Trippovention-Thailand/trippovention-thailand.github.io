Tour Overview & Description

An unforgettable afternoon-into-night voyage across Krabi's premier islands

Experience one of Krabi's most popular island-hopping adventures as you explore seven stunning islands by speedboat. Departing at midday, cruise through the crystal-clear waters of the Andaman Sea, stopping at picturesque islands and secluded bays for swimming, snorkeling, and relaxation away from early morning peak crowds.

Visit iconic destinations including Chicken Island, Tup Island, Poda Island, Railay Beach, Yawasam Island, Koh Tan Ming, and the famous Phra Nang Cave Beach. As dusk descends, witness a breathtaking tropical sunset over the Andaman Sea before enjoying a delicious beachside dinner. Conclude this magical evening with a swim among glowing bioluminescent plankton (weather and sea conditions permitting), creating lifelong memories.

Places Covered

The complete list of seven islands and iconic landmarks on this tour

Islands & Reefs

Archipelago Exploration

  • Koh Poda: Iconic limestone tower, broad beach, and tranquil Andaman turquoise waters.
  • Chicken Island (Koh Kai): Spectacular poultry-shaped rock tower with teeming coral gardens.
  • Tup Island (Talay Waek): Walk across the natural sandbar bridging Tup Island and Chicken Island.
  • Yawasam Island & Koh Tan Ming: Prime deep-water snorkeling sites with dramatic sea life and corals.
Sunset & Night Wonder

Beaches & Bioluminescence

  • Phra Nang Cave Beach: Stalactites, limestone walls, emerald sea, and sacred Princess Cave.
  • Railay Beach: World-renowned rock climber paradise and gorgeous sunset vantage point.
  • Bioluminescent Plankton Swim: Experience the magical glowing waters after dark under starry skies.
  • Beachside Dinner: Savor a delicious Thai dinner right by the sunset-lit seashore.
