Tour Overview & Description

Weekend Walking Street, Authentic Southern Street Delicacies, Live Music & Artisan Crafts

Experience the colorful evening energy of Krabi Town's famous Walking Street market (Thanon Khon Dern). When twilight falls, the central streets of the provincial capital burst into vibrant life with hundreds of food stalls, artisan craft makers, and local street performers.

Accompanied by a knowledgeable local foodie guide, you will wander through bustling lanes sampling Southern Thai street delights: crispy rotis with condensed milk, charcoal-grilled chicken satay with peanut sauce, spicy som tum papaya salad, fresh seafood skewers, and coconut ice cream. Enjoy live music on the public stage and shop for handmade jewelry and local souvenirs.

Places Covered & Highlights

Experience the highlights and sights included on this journey

Evening Culture

Night Market Sights

  • Krabi Walking Street: The famous pedestrian night bazaar operating every Friday, Saturday, and Sunday evening.
  • Live Stage Entertainment: Enjoy local Thai musicians, youth dance troupes, and acoustic acts in the town square.
  • Handcrafted Souvenirs: Browse batik sarongs, hand-carved coconut crafts, paintings, and handmade jewelry.
Flavors of Krabi

Street Food Delights

  • Crispy Thai Roti & Satay: Warm freshly fried banana rotis and tender chicken satay grilled over charcoal.
  • Southern Thai Curries & Noodles: Sample fiery Gaeng Som curries and fresh rice noodles with herb accompaniments.
  • Exotic Tropical Desserts: Taste fresh coconut ice cream served inside real coconut shells with sweet sticky rice.
