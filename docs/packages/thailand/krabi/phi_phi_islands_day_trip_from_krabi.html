Tour Overview & Description

High-Speed Speedboat Cruise, Maya Bay, Emerald Pileh Lagoon, Viking Cave & Bamboo Island

Cross the open Andaman Sea on a modern high-speed speedboat to Thailand's most renowned island destination: Phi Phi Leh and Phi Phi Don. Experience sheer limestone crags towering hundreds of meters above azure waters.

Walk upon the pristine sands of world-famous Maya Bay, step into the calm emerald pool of Pileh Lagoon, gaze at Viking Cave swifts' nest harvesting scaffolds, and encounter curious macaques at Monkey Beach. Indulge in an international buffet lunch on Phi Phi Don followed by coral reef snorkeling off powdery Bamboo Island.

Places Covered & Highlights

Experience the highlights and sights included on this journey

World Famous

Phi Phi Leh Highlights

  • Maya Bay: The world-famous bay made legendary by 'The Beach', strictly protected and offering powder-soft white sand and turquoise shallows.
  • Pileh Lagoon: A breathtaking emerald-green natural swimming pool enclosed by sheer 100-meter karst cliffs.
  • Viking Cave: Historic limestone cavern with prehistoric paintings and active bird's nest harvesting perches.
Beaches & Reefs

Phi Phi Don & Bamboo Island

  • Monkey Beach: Lush coastal cove inhabited by playful wild crab-eating macaque monkeys.
  • Phi Phi Don Town: The main inhabited island where you will enjoy a hearty buffet lunch in a seaside restaurant.
  • Bamboo Island (Koh Mai Phai): A flat uninhabited coral island ringed by pristine white sand beaches and thriving shallow reefs.
