Tour Overview & Description

A rejuvenating natural eco-excursion into Krabi's lowland rainforest reserve

Escape into the natural beauty of Krabi with a relaxing visit to the famous Hot Springs and the stunning Emerald Pool (Sa Morakot). Begin your journey at the natural hot springs, where warm mineral-rich geothermal waters cascade through smooth limestone tiers surrounded by lush tropical rainforest.

Continue to the Emerald Pool, a crystal-clear natural freshwater lagoon renowned for its striking turquoise and emerald waters, perfect for a refreshing swim after a scenic jungle walk. Surrounded by tropical greenery, elevated wooden boardwalk trails, and tranquil flora, this tour offers the ideal combination of relaxation, wellness, and outdoor nature exploration for families, couples, and nature lovers alike.

Places Covered

Natural thermal wonders and pristine forest reserves visited on this day trip

Thermal Wellness

Krabi Hot Springs Waterfall

  • Natural Hot Springs (Namtok Ron): Therapeutic geothermal spring pools naturally warmed between 35°C to 40°C.
  • Cascading Stream: Smooth hollowed-out limestone rock tubs providing a relaxing natural jacuzzi experience.
  • Mineral Benefits: Natural volcanic salts that soothe sore muscles, rejuvenate skin, and promote circulation.
Forest Lagoon

Emerald Pool Nature Reserve

  • Sa Morakot (Emerald Pool): Famous natural mineral spring lake reflecting vibrant green and aquamarine colors.
  • Rainforest Walking Trail: 1.4-kilometer elevated wooden boardwalk winding past crystal-clear streams and rare trees.
  • Blue Lagoon / Blue Pool: Deep indigo geothermal pond nestled further into the rainforest (viewing point).
