Tour Overview & Description

An ethical and enriching morning connecting with rescued Asian elephants

Spend a memorable morning at an ethical elephant sanctuary, where you'll interact closely with rescued elephants in their natural habitat. Begin with a welcome soft drink and an insightful introduction to traditional rubber tree cultivation before walking through the lush jungle alongside these gentle giants.

Feed the elephants nutritious treats, enjoy a fun mud spa, help bathe them in fresh natural water, and freshen them up with an open-air shower. The experience also includes a hands-on Pad Thai cooking session, offering a perfect blend of ethical wildlife care, nature exploration, and authentic Thai culinary culture for visitors of all ages.

Places Covered

Key areas and cultural activity zones visited during the morning program

Sanctuary Habitat

Nature & Wildlife Highlights

  • Elephant Sanctuary: A peaceful, cruelty-free sanctuary providing lifelong care to rescued Asian elephants.
  • Rubber Plantation: Traditional southern Thai rubber tree cultivation demonstration and sap collection.
  • Jungle Trail: Scenic walking trail alongside elephants exploring rich native vegetation and forested hills.
Interactive Care

Hands-On Experiences

  • Mud Spa & Bathing: Apply cooling therapeutic mud onto elephant skin and wash them down in the bathing pond.
  • Feeding Session: Hand-feed seasonal fruits, sugar cane, and dietary supplements directly to the elephants.
  • Traditional Cooking Area: Interactive step-by-step masterclass preparing authentic, delicious Thai Pad Thai.
