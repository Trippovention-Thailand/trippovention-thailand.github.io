Tour Overview & Description

Off-Road Quad Biking, Rugged Jungle Trails, Plantation Paths & Scenic Limestone Viewpoints

Experience an adrenaline-pumping off-road expedition in Krabi with an ATV quad ride through dramatic natural terrain. Drive through tropical jungles, dirt paths, muddy tracks, and palm plantations inaccessible to standard vehicles.

Following an experienced trail marshal, you will ascend scenic elevated dirt paths leading to secluded viewpoints overlooking Krabi's majestic limestone karsts and countryside valleys. A thrilling outdoor activity suitable for adventure enthusiasts, couples, and active families.

Places Covered & Highlights

Experience the highlights and sights included on this journey

Adrenaline Rush

Off-Road Trails

  • Jungle Dirt Tracks & Creeks: Navigate varied terrain featuring muddy ruts, gravel inclines, and natural forest paths.
  • Palm Oil & Rubber Plantations: Cruise past rows of tall tropical trees on private off-road agricultural acreage.
  • Hillside Viewpoint Climb: Reach panoramic ridges offering wide vistas over Krabi's limestone towers and valleys.
Modern Quads

Safety & Machinery

  • Automatic 4-Stroke ATVs: Modern automatic quad bikes that are easy to handle and require no previous gear-shifting experience.
  • Practice Circuit Run: Safety briefing and test loop to ensure you feel 100% confident with throttle and brakes before hitting the trails.
  • Professional Trail Guide: Lead and sweep guides ensure rider safety, pacing, and assistance throughout the route.
