Frequently Asked Questions

Important information regarding pickups, booking workflow, national park charges & seasonality

How does the enquiry and booking workflow work?▾

Clicking "Enquire Now" on any card automatically routes you to Trippovention’s existing enquiry form, prefilling your selected activity and destination. Our travel coordinators will review availability, arrange tailored timings, and provide official confirmation with no hidden booking fees.

What is the difference between Krabi 4 Islands and Hong Islands?▾

The 4 Islands tour visits Koh Poda, Chicken Island, the famous Tup Island sandbar (Talay Waek), and Phra Nang Cave Beach. The Hong Islands tour travels northwest into Than Bok Khorani National Park, focusing on the enclosed emerald Hong Lagoon, Lading Island, and 360° panoramic viewpoints.

Are National Park fees included in Krabi day tours?▾

National Park entrance fees for island marine tours (THB 200–300 per adult, THB 100–150 per child) are collected in cash directly on-site by government park rangers. Our inland tours like the Hot Springs & Emerald Pool include all reserve admission fees upfront.

Can I request a private boat instead of a shared group tour?▾

Yes! We offer private longtail boat and private speedboat charters for couples, families, and private groups. Simply choose the private tour option or specify your request on the enquiry form.
