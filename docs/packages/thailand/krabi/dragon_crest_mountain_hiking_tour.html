Tour Overview & Description

Challenging 3.7 km Rainforest Trek, 565m Summit Overlook, Legendary Hanging Rock & 360° Panorama

Khao Ngon Nak (Dragon Crest Mountain) is heralded by international travelers and outdoor photographers as having the single most spectacular panoramic viewpoint in all of Southern Thailand. Located within Than Bok Khorani National Park, the trailhead begins in peaceful Tubkaek Beach.

Ascend a 3.7-kilometer jungle trail through pristine virgin rainforest, passing small cascades and ancient fig trees. Upon reaching the 565-meter summit plateau, you will be rewarded with a mind-blowing 360-degree panorama: the emerald Andaman Sea, Phang Nga Bay islands, and dramatic karst mountains stretching all the way to the horizon.

Places Covered & Highlights

Experience the highlights and sights included on this journey

360° Wonder

The Summit Viewpoints

  • 565-Meter Summit Ridge: Stand above the clouds with uninterrupted views sweeping from Hong Islands across to Phi Phi and Ao Nang.
  • The Hanging Rock Ledge: The legendary rock outcrop suspended over the vertical cliff abyss—the ultimate Krabi photo backdrop.
  • Sunrise / Morning Golden Hour: Early departures catch cool mountain mist drifting across emerald canopy valleys.
3.7 km Trek

Rainforest Trail

  • Native Dipterocarp Canopy: Shaded jungle trail with wooden steps, natural roots, and resting stations every 500 meters.
  • Waterfalls & Springs: Pass small freshwater stream waypoints along the mid-mountain sections.
  • Guided Pace & Flora Beta: Knowledgeable local trekking guide ensuring safe pacing, wildlife spotting, and hydration.
