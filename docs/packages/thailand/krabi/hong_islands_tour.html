Tour Overview & Description

An unforgettable island-hopping journey into Than Bok Khorani National Park

Discover the breathtaking beauty of the Hong Islands on an exciting speedboat adventure through the crystal-clear waters of the Andaman Sea. Explore pristine white-sand beaches, hidden lagoons, and dramatic limestone cliffs while enjoying opportunities to swim, snorkel, and relax amidst unspoiled natural surroundings.

Visit the iconic Hong Lagoon, known for its emerald-green waters enclosed by towering cliffs accessed through a narrow sea entrance, and spend leisure time soaking in the tranquil atmosphere of Hong Island Beach. A delicious lunch box and seasonal fruits are included, making this tour an ideal choice for nature lovers, families, couples, and adventure seekers looking to experience one of Krabi's most picturesque island destinations.

Places Covered

Stunning islands and coastal highlights visited during the Hong archipelago tour

Signature Highlights

Hong Island & Hidden Lagoon

  • Hong Island (Koh Hong): The main island famous for Pelay Beach, fine powdery white sand, and shade beneath limestone cliffs.
  • Hong Lagoon: A mesmerizing enclosed emerald-green seawater chamber with towering sheer cliffs and a dramatic cliff entrance.
  • Hong Viewpoint: Optional panoramic 360-degree staircase trail offering spectacular bird's-eye views over Phang Nga Bay.
Satellite Islands

Lading & Pakbia Islands

  • Lading Island (Paradise Island): A secluded bay bordered by limestone formations, celebrated for peaceful swimming and swallow-nest harvesting history.
  • Pakbia Island: Known for quiet sands, unique rock formations, and low-tide sandbars connecting to neighboring islets.
  • Andaman Snorkeling Spots: Clear coastal waters filled with vibrant coral heads, butterflyfish, and clownfish.
