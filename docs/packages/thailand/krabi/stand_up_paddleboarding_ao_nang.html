Tour Overview & Description

Sunset Coastal Paddling, Calming Andaman Waters, Limestone Sea Cliffs & Action Photos

Discover the tranquil pleasure of stand-up paddleboarding (SUP) along the stunning coastal waters of Ao Nang and Pai Plong Bay. Gliding standing up on calm water gives you an elevated, crystal-clear perspective of marine life and the seabed below.

Under the friendly guidance of a certified SUP coach, beginners will quickly master mounting, balancing, and stroke techniques. The tour departs in the late afternoon to catch the magical golden hour, paddling beneath soaring limestone cliffs as the sun dips below the Andaman horizon.

Paddleboarding Highlights

  • Pai Plong & Ao Nang Bays: Paddle along dramatic limestone headlands framing secluded natural sandy coves.
  • Golden Hour Sunset Horizon: Experience sunset directly from the water with reflections painting the sea in gold and bronze.
  • Gentle Full-Body Workout: Build core balance and fitness effortlessly in a calm, meditative outdoor setting.
Top Quality

Coaching & Equipment

  • High-Stability Inflatable SUPs: Wide, buoyant, premium inflatable boards suited for first-timers and intermediate paddlers alike.
  • Certified SUP Instructor: Quick 15-minute shore clinic followed by gentle on-water coaching.
  • Waterproof Phone Case & Photos: Instructor captures beautiful candid action photos of your sunset session.
