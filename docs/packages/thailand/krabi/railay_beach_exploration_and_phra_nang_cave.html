Tour Overview & Description

Iconic Coastal Day Trip, Railay West, Phra Nang Princess Shrine, Limestone Karsts & Longtail Transit

Railay is a world unto itself: a secluded tropical peninsula cut off from the Krabi mainland by impassable, soaring limestone mountains, accessible solely by sea. It feels more like a magical offshore island than a mainland coast.

Hop on a scenic longtail boat shuttle from Ao Nang across emerald waves. Stroll along Railay West's sweeping crescent of white sand, wander through shaded limestone pathways linking to Railay East, and discover the mysterious stalactite-draped Princess Cave (Tham Phra Nang), revered by local sea gypsies and fishermen for good fortune.

Places Covered & Highlights

Experience the highlights and sights included on this journey

World Famous

Railay Peninsula Highlights

  • Railay West Beach: Expansive powdery beach bordered by vertical karst cliffs, emerald waters, and beachside cafes.
  • Phra Nang Cave Beach: Voted among the world's most beautiful beaches, famous for sacred fisherman shrines and clear swimming.
  • Stalactite Caverns: Gaze up at dramatic limestone dripstone formations, caves, and overhangs right on the beach.
Relax & Explore

Atmosphere & Activities

  • Rock Climbing Spectacle: Watch international sport climbers scale sheer cliffs directly over the beach.
  • Jungle Trail Walking: Paved footpaths connect beaches under the shade of limestone bluffs and wild banyan trees.
  • Relaxed Island Vibe: Completely car-free environment with relaxed bohemian cafes and tropical juice bars.
