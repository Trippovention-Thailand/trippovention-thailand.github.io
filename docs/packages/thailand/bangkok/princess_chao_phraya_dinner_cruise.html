Cruise Overview

Experience Bangkok's magical illuminated skyline from the majestic Chao Phraya River

Enjoy a luxurious evening aboard the Chao Phraya River with the Princess Dinner Cruise, offering stunning views of Bangkok’s illuminated landmarks. Sail past iconic sites like Wat Arun and the Grand Palace while enjoying a delicious international buffet dinner. Experience live music and entertainment in a romantic and relaxing ambiance. This cruise is a perfect way to explore Bangkok’s nightlife with scenic river views.

Cruise Highlights

What makes Princess Chao Phraya Dinner Cruise an unforgettable night

River Dining

International Buffet Dinner

Savor a sumptuous buffet offering both vegetarian and non-vegetarian selections, including fresh seafood, authentic Thai dishes, sushi, salads, and indulgent desserts prepared by skilled chefs.

Nighttime Splendor

Illuminated Landmarks & Live Music

Cruise past floodlit monuments including the Temple of Dawn (Wat Arun), Grand Palace, and Rama VIII Bridge, accompanied by lively live music and talented singers creating a wonderful atmosphere.
