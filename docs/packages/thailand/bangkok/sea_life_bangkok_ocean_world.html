Tour Overview & Description

Southeast Asia's Largest Aquarium, 270° Ocean Tunnel, Shark Walk & Living Coral Reefs

Located in the heart of downtown Bangkok beneath the luxury Siam Paragon shopping complex, SEA LIFE Bangkok Ocean World is Southeast Asia's largest indoor aquarium. Spanning over 10,000 square meters, it houses more than 30,000 marine creatures representing 400 species from around the world.

Walk through the spectacular 270-degree underwater ocean tunnel where Sand Tiger sharks, giant stingrays, and schools of trevally glide gracefully overhead. Discover 11 immersive zones, from the magical Coral Reef to the Antarctic Penguin Ice Adventure and interactive Touch Pool.

Places Covered & Highlights

Experience the highlights and sights included on this journey

Marine Highlights

Deep Sea Encounters

  • 270° Ocean Tunnel: Walk along the ocean floor as giant sand tiger sharks, eagle rays, and moray eels swim all around you.
  • Shark Walk: Step courageously across a transparent acrylic glass floor with five species of sharks swimming directly beneath your feet.
  • Open Ocean Feeding Show: Watch professional scuba divers hand-feed stingrays and sharks in the massive 8-meter-deep main tank.
Themed Zones

Wildlife Habitats

  • Penguin Ice Adventure: Observe charming Gentoo and African penguins waddling on snow banks and diving through sub-zero waters.
  • Living Coral Reef: Vibrant underwater garden showcasing clownfish, sea anemones, lionfish, and delicate seahorses.
  • Rainforest & Giant Otters: Walk through lush indoor jungle paths to spot Asian small-clawed otters and freshwater Amazon stingrays.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about this Bangkok activity

How do I get to SEA LIFE Bangkok Ocean World?▾

Take the BTS SkyTrain to BTS Siam Station (interchange station). Take Exit 5 directly into Siam Paragon shopping mall and descend to basement level B1/B2.

Can I combine SEA LIFE Bangkok with Madame Tussauds?▾

Yes! We offer a popular combo ticket that includes both SEA LIFE Bangkok and Madame Tussauds at nearby Siam Discovery at a discounted rate.

Is the aquarium fully wheelchair and stroller accessible?▾

Yes, the entire facility is equipped with gentle ramps, wide viewing tunnels, and elevator access suitable for strollers and wheelchairs.

How much time should I allocate for the visit?▾

Most families and visitors spend between 2 and 2.5 hours exploring the 11 zones and watching the diver feeding demonstrations.
