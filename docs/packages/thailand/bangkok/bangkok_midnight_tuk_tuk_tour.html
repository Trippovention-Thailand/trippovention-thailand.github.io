Open-Air Tuk Tuk Ride, Legendary Street Food Stalls, Flower Market & Wat Pho at Night

Tour Overview & Description

Open-Air Tuk Tuk Ride, Legendary Street Food Stalls, Flower Market & Wat Pho at Night

When darkness falls, Bangkok transforms into a buzzing culinary wonderland. Hop aboard an iconic motorized three-wheeled Tuk Tuk and zoom through illuminated avenues, historic alleyways, and royal boulevards to uncover the city's legendary nighttime street food institutions.

Led by an expert English-speaking local foodie guide, you will savor award-winning Pad Thai, aromatic Guay Tiew Kua Gai (crispy fried chicken noodles), handmade dim sum, and sweet mango sticky rice. Conclude the evening wandering through the peaceful, floodlit courtyards of Wat Pho and the bustling 24-hour Flower Market.

Places Covered & Highlights

Experience the highlights and sights included on this journey

Michelin Tastings

Street Food Tastings

  • Legendary Pad Thai Stall: Taste iconic wok-charred Pad Thai wrapped in delicate egg ribbons at a celebrated heritage vendor.
  • Crispy Chicken Flat Noodles: Watch street chefs sear wide rice noodles over blazing charcoal flames in Chinatown.
  • Mango Sticky Rice & Sweets: Indulge in sweet golden mangoes paired with fragrant warm coconut sticky rice and roasted mung beans.
Illuminated Sights

Night Sightseeing

  • Wat Pho by Night: Experience the serene, tranquil magic of the Temple of the Reclining Buddha's illuminated golden stupas without daytime crowds.
  • Pak Khlong Talad (Flower Market): Walk through Thailand's largest 24-hour wholesale flower market bursting with fresh orchids, jasmine, and marigold garlands.
  • Secret Rooftop River View: Sip a refreshing beverage overlooking the illuminated Wat Arun across the tranquil Chao Phraya River.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about this Bangkok activity

Are vegetarian or dietary restrictions accommodated?▾

Yes, we can readily accommodate vegetarians, non-pork eaters, and mild spice preferences. Please notify us during your enquiry.

Can children participate in the night tuk-tuk tour?▾

Yes, older children and teens love riding in open-air tuk-tuks! Families with young children can also request car or minivan transport if preferred.

Is the food sanitary and safe to eat?▾

All tasting stops are carefully vetted by our culinary team, featuring busy heritage stalls with high ingredient turnover and Michelin Bib Gourmand recognitions.

Is hotel drop-off included at the end of the tour?▾

Yes, at the conclusion of the evening (around 11:00 PM), your tuk-tuk driver will drop you off directly at your central Bangkok hotel lobby.
