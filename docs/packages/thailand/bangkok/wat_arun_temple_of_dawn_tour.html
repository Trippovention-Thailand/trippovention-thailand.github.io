Tour Overview & Description

Iconic Riverside Khmer-Style Prang, Porcelain Mosaics & Chao Phraya Cross-River Ferry

Perched on the west bank of the Chao Phraya River in Thonburi, Wat Arun Ratchawararam (commonly known as the Temple of Dawn) is one of Thailand's most striking visual landmarks. Named after Aruna, the Hindu god of dawn, its central prang catches the morning sunrise and glows dramatically at twilight.

Ascend the steep steps of the central 70-meter-high Khmer-style tower, encrusted with thousands of pieces of colorful glazed Chinese porcelain and sea shells that once served as ballast on trade junks. Take in panoramic vistas over the winding Chao Phraya River, the Grand Palace skyline, and historic riverside neighborhoods.

Places Covered & Highlights

Experience the highlights and sights included on this journey

Riverside Icon

Architectural Highlights

  • Central Prang Spire: Majestic 70-meter tower representing Mount Meru, the center of the universe in Buddhist and Hindu cosmology.
  • Glazed Porcelain Mosaics: Fascinating floral mosaics crafted from antique Chinese ceramics, broken porcelain shards, and seashells.
  • Guardian Giants (Yak): Two towering mythical daemon sentinels guarding the entrance to the ordination hall.
Local Life

Cultural Experience

  • Cross-River Ferry Experience: Short scenic river crossing aboard the traditional wooden commuter ferry between Tha Tien and Wat Arun.
  • Thai Traditional Costume Rental: Popular local photo experience with optional traditional Thai silk outfits available near temple entrance.
  • Riverside Ordination Chapel: Peaceful inner courtyard featuring murals depicting Prince Siddhartha's life.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about this Bangkok activity

Can you climb the steep steps at Wat Arun?▾

Visitors can climb to the first terrace of the central prang for closer inspection of the intricate porcelain tiles and river views. Upper terraces are cordoned off for structural preservation.

What is the best time of day to visit Wat Arun?▾

Early mornings (08:30–10:00 AM) offer cooler temperatures and softer light for photography, while late afternoons allow you to experience the riverside as the sunset casts a golden hue over the river.

Is traditional Thai costume rental available on-site?▾

Yes, numerous licensed costume rental boutiques operate just outside the temple gates offering traditional silk Chut Thai outfits and accessories for photos.

How do we cross the river to get there?▾

Our tour includes the charming local river ferry from Tha Tien pier directly across to the temple dock, offering classic waterfront photo angles along the way.
