Tour Overview & Description

World-Famous Train Track Market, Retracting Awnings & Damnoen Saduak Boat Ride

Combine Thailand's two most extraordinary and legendary traditional markets in an unforgettable full-day excursion. Witness the sensational spectacle of the Maeklong Railway Market (Talad Rom Hup), followed by the vibrant sights, sounds, and aromas of Damnoen Saduak Floating Market.

At Maeklong, watch vendors calmly pull back their canvas awnings and baskets of produce within seconds as a rumbling passenger train brushes mere inches past market stalls. Then transfer to Damnoen Saduak to board a traditional wooden boat and navigate the bustling trading canals.

Places Covered & Highlights

Experience the highlights and sights included on this journey

Train Track Miracle

Maeklong Railway Market

  • Folding Umbrella Spectacle: Watch market stalls retract awnings and clear the tracks as the siren wails and the passenger train crawls past.
  • Railway Track Walk: Stroll directly along the active railway tracks lined with fresh seafood, tropical durians, and spices.
  • Local Train Station: Historic terminal station where travelers can capture photos alongside the classic diesel locomotive.
Canal Adventure

Damnoen Saduak Market

  • Paddle Boat Cruise: Glide through the lively trading canals aboard a wooden longtail boat surrounded by vendor canoes.
  • Canal Street Food: Sample freshly cooked mango sticky rice, grilled chicken skewers, and sweet coconut pancakes.
  • Coconut Sugar Workshop: Observe traditional palm sugar boiling over clay woodfired kilns.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about this Bangkok activity

Will we definitely see the train pass through the market?▾

Yes, our tour itinerary is precisely scheduled around the State Railway of Thailand train arrival times so you can safely watch and photograph the market awnings retract.

Is it safe standing near the train tracks?▾

Yes, the train travels at a slow walking speed (approx. 15 km/h) through the market, sounding its horn continuously while shopkeepers and our guide ensure all guests stand safely behind the yellow marker lines.

Can we ride the train itself?▾

The standard tour views the train passing from the market tracks for optimal photography. An optional upgrade to ride one station into Maeklong can be arranged on private tours.

How much time is spent at each market?▾

Approximately 1 to 1.5 hours at Maeklong Railway Market, followed by 1.5 to 2 hours at Damnoen Saduak Floating Market, including the boat ride and free time for lunch.
