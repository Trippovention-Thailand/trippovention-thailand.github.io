Thailand Island & City Explorer

Experience the best of Thailand with vibrant city life in Bangkok, the lively beaches of Pattaya, and the tropical beauty of Phuket's world-famous islands and scenic bays.

🗓️

Duration

10 Days / 9 Nights

💰

Price Per Person

Ideal For

Detailed Itinerary

Day 1

Arrival in Bangkok & Transfer to Pattaya

  • Welcome to Thailand's Beach Paradise
  • Arrive at Bangkok Airport and complete immigration formalities.
  • Meet & greet by our local representative.
  • Transfer to Pattaya by private vehicle.
  • Check in at the hotel.
  • Relax after your journey and enjoy an overnight stay in Pattaya.
Day 2

Coral Island Speedboat Tour

  • A Tropical Escape
  • Breakfast at the hotel.
  • Proceed for the Coral Island Tour by Speedboat.
  • Relax on pristine beaches or enjoy optional water sports.
  • Enjoy lunch during the tour.
  • Return to the hotel in Pattaya.
Meals: Breakfast & Lunch
Tickets: Included
Day 3

Transfer to Bangkok

  • From Beachside Bliss to the Capital City
  • Breakfast at the hotel.
  • Check out from the hotel.
  • Transfer to Bangkok by private vehicle.
  • Check in at the hotel.
  • Overnight stay in Bangkok.
Meals: Breakfast
Day 4

Leisure Day in Bangkok

  • Explore the City at Your Own Pace
  • Breakfast at the hotel.
  • Spend the day at leisure.
  • Explore Bangkok's shopping malls, local markets, cafés, or choose optional sightseeing activities.
  • Overnight stay in Bangkok.
Meals: Breakfast
Day 5

Fly to Phuket

  • Welcome to Thailand's Island Paradise
  • Breakfast at the hotel.
  • Check out from the hotel.
  • Transfer to Bangkok Airport.
  • Board your flight to Phuket (flight not included).
  • Upon arrival, transfer to your hotel by private vehicle.
  • Check in and relax.
Meals: Breakfast
Day 6

Phi Phi Island Speedboat Tour

  • Discover Thailand's Most Famous Islands
  • Breakfast at the hotel.
  • Proceed for the Phi Phi Island Tour by Speedboat.
  • Explore breathtaking beaches, turquoise waters, and scenic viewpoints.
  • Enjoy lunch during the tour.
  • Return to the hotel.
Meals: Breakfast & Lunch
Tickets: Included
Day 7

Phang Nga Bay & Little Amazon Tour

  • Nature's Hidden Wonders
  • Breakfast at the hotel.
  • Proceed for a Phang Nga Bay & Little Amazon Tour.
  • Cruise through dramatic limestone cliffs and mangrove forests.
  • Discover the unique natural beauty of the Little Amazon.
  • Enjoy lunch during the excursion.
  • Return to the hotel.
Meals: Breakfast & Lunch
Tickets: Included
Day 8

James Bond Island by Speedboat & Canoe

  • Adventure Through Iconic Landscapes
  • Breakfast at the hotel.
  • Proceed for the James Bond Island Tour by Speedboat & Canoe.
  • Explore the famous limestone islands featured in the James Bond movie.
  • Enjoy canoeing through sea caves and hidden lagoons.
  • Lunch during the tour.
  • Return to the hotel.
Meals: Breakfast & Lunch
Tickets: Included
Day 9

Leisure Day in Phuket

  • Relax & Explore
  • Breakfast at the hotel.
  • Spend the day at leisure.
  • Relax on the beach, explore local markets, or choose from optional sightseeing and water activities.
  • Overnight stay in Phuket.
Meals: Breakfast
Day 10

Departure

  • Farewell Thailand
  • Breakfast at the hotel.
  • Check out from the hotel.
  • Private transfer to Phuket International Airport.
  • Depart with unforgettable memories of Thailand.
Meals: Breakfast
