Tour Overview & Description

Interactive Celebrity Wax Museum at Siam Discovery, World Leaders, Sports Stars & Pop Icons

Step onto the red carpet and mingle with the world's most famous icons at Madame Tussauds Bangkok, located on the 4th floor of the ultra-modern Siam Discovery shopping center. Unlike traditional museums, there are no velvet ropes or barriers—you can get up close, pose, touch, and photograph every figure.

Spanning eight interactive zones, encounter lifelike wax figures of global political leaders, legendary historical figures, Hollywood movie stars, Grammy-winning pop musicians, sports champions, and celebrated Thai film stars. Complete your visit with fun themed photo props and interactive video stations.

History & Sports Stars

  • Leaders & Royal Court: Stand in the Oval Office beside world presidents or sit with Mahatma Gandhi and the Dalai Lama.

  • Wax-Making Exhibition: Fascinating behind-the-scenes display revealing how sculptors create lifelike wax masterpieces over hundreds of hours.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about this Bangkok activity

Can you touch the wax figures and take photos with them?▾

Yes! Madame Tussauds Bangkok is completely interactive. There are no velvet ropes, so visitors are encouraged to touch, pose, hug, and photograph every figure.

How do I get to Siam Discovery?▾

Take the BTS SkyTrain to BTS Siam or BTS National Stadium. Walk across the skybridge straight into Siam Discovery and take the escalators to the 4th floor.

Is there an age limit for children?▾

Madame Tussauds is welcoming to visitors of all ages. Children under 3 years old enter free of charge.

Can I buy a combo ticket with SEA LIFE Bangkok?▾

Yes, we offer an excellent-value Twin Attractions Combo pass combining Madame Tussauds and SEA LIFE Bangkok Ocean World, located just minutes apart in Siam.
