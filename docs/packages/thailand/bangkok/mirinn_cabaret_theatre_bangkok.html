Tour Overview & Description

Vibrant Thai Dance, Muay Thai Demonstrations, Spectacular Cabaret & Extravagant Staging

Mirinn Cabaret Theatre delivers a unique and dynamic theatrical fusion that sets it apart from traditional cabaret shows in Thailand. Combining glamorous international cabaret choreography with authentic Muay Thai martial arts demonstrations and graceful classical Thai royal dances, Mirinn offers an action-packed 70-minute spectacle.

Enjoy lavish costume changes, aerial stunts, live boxing choreography, and modern sound-and-light production at the dedicated theatre at RCA. The show celebrates Thai heritage alongside energetic international music tracks from around the globe.

Places Covered & Highlights

Experience the highlights and sights included on this journey

Muay Thai Action

Martial Arts & Heritage

  • Muay Thai Boxing Choreography: Thrilling live exhibition of ancient Muay Boran techniques and dynamic modern kickboxing stage fights.
  • Classical Thai Kinnaree Dance: Graceful performance depicting mythical half-bird, half-human celestial dancers from Thai mythology.
  • Sword & Staff Martial Arts: Acrobatic ancient weapons demonstrations set to pulsating traditional drums.
World Variety

Glamorous Cabaret

  • International Pop Revues: High-energy dance spectacles featuring popular hits from K-Pop, Hollywood, and Bollywood.
  • Elaborate Feather Costumes: Spectacular handmade crystal headpieces, iridescent silk robes, and dramatic feather backdrops.
  • Cast Meet & Greet: Lively photo session with the cast and martial artists immediately following the finale.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about this Bangkok activity

What makes Mirinn Theatre different from other cabarets?▾

Mirinn is unique because it integrates authentic Muay Thai kickboxing and martial arts acrobatics directly into its glamorous cabaret and cultural dance acts.

Is dinner available at the theatre?▾

Yes, you can upgrade your booking to include a delicious Thai and international buffet dinner served prior to showtime in the theatre's adjacent dining salon.

Is the show suitable for children and families?▾

Yes, the performance is completely family-oriented, featuring acrobatic stunts, cultural music, and energetic dance numbers that appeal to all ages.

Where is Mirinn Theatre located?▾

Mirinn is situated at RCA Plaza on Rama 9 Road, one of Bangkok's central entertainment and dining hubs, accessible via MRT Phetchaburi.
