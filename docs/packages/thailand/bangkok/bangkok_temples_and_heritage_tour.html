Tour Overview & Description

Wat Traimit (Solid Gold Buddha), Wat Pho Reclining Buddha & Marble Temple Wat Benchamabophit

Immerse yourself in Bangkok's rich religious heritage and architectural wonders on this curated half-day temple expedition. Discover three of the capital's most significant sanctuaries, each representing a distinct era of royal Thai craftsmanship and Buddhist devotion.

Stand in awe before the world's largest solid gold Buddha statue at Wat Traimit in Chinatown, witness the majestic 46-meter Reclining Buddha at Wat Pho, and admire the immaculate white Italian Carrara marble architecture of Wat Benchamabophit (The Marble Temple).

Places Covered & Highlights

Experience the highlights and sights included on this journey

Sacred Highlights

Chinatown & Old City

  • Wat Traimit (Golden Buddha): Home to the world's largest solid gold statue—weighing 5.5 tons and dating back to the Sukhothai period.
  • Yaowarat Gateway: Scenic drive past Bangkok Chinatown's monumental ceremonial Chinese gate and vibrant goldsmith district.
  • Wat Pho Reclining Buddha: Grand 46-meter gilded Buddha image and historic royal chedis.
Marble Masterpiece

Dusit Royal District

  • Wat Benchamabophit (Marble Temple): Exquisite late-19th-century temple faced with imported white Italian Carrara marble.
  • Cloister Gallery of 52 Buddhas: Open courtyard housing 52 bronze Buddha statues showcasing different Asian artistic periods.
  • Government District Drive: Scenic drive past Ananta Samakhom Throne Hall, Dusit Palace, and Rajadamnern Avenue.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about this Bangkok activity

Are all temple admission tickets included?▾

Yes, admission fees for all three temples (Wat Traimit, Wat Pho, and Wat Benchamabophit) are completely included in your package.

Is hotel pickup available across all Bangkok areas?▾

Complimentary pickup is provided for hotels in Sukhumvit (Soi 1–33 & 2–26), Silom, Sathorn, Siam, and Pratunam. Outlying pickups can be arranged for a modest supplement.

How long do we spend at each temple?▾

Typically 45 to 60 minutes at each temple, giving plenty of time for detailed guide explanations, photo opportunities, and respectful contemplation.

What should I wear for this heritage tour?▾

Dress respectfully with covered shoulders and knees. Shorts, sleeveless vests, and short skirts are strictly forbidden at active Buddhist temples.
