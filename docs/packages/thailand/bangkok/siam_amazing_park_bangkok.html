Tour Overview & Description

World's Largest Wave Pool, 5 Themed Amusement Zones, Giant Water Slides & International Buffet

Established in 1980 and formerly known as Siam Park City, Siam Amazing Park is Thailand's largest amusement and water theme park. Certified by Guinness World Records for housing the world's largest wave pool (spanning 13,600 square meters), it offers a full day of thrilling escapism for families and adventure seekers.

The expansive 120-acre complex is divided into five themed zones: Water World, X-Zone, Family World, Fantasy World, and Small World. Experience high-speed adrenaline on the 33-meter Speed Slide, loop upside down on the suspended Vortex coaster, or relax under coconut palms along the artificial golden sand beach.

Places Covered & Highlights

Experience the highlights and sights included on this journey

World Record Pool

Water World Zone

  • Guinness Record Wave Pool: Enormous 13,600 sq. meter artificial sea creating gentle ocean swells and rolling waves.
  • 7-Story Speed Slide: Rainbow-colored 33-meter high rainbow water slide certified as one of the tallest in Southeast Asia.
  • Flowing River & Spa Pool: Lazy meandering river and bubbling hot tub lagoons surrounded by tropical greenery.
Thrill Rides

Adventure Theme Zones

  • Vortex Suspended Coaster: 765-meter inverted looping roller coaster reaching speeds of up to 80 km/h.
  • Boomerang & Giant Drop: Forward-and-backward looping coaster and a 75-meter vertical free-fall tower.
  • Si-Am Tower: 109-meter revolving observation tower offering 360-degree aerial views across Bangkok.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about this Bangkok activity

Are towels and lockers available for rent at the park?▾

Yes, clean towels, swim rings, and secure electronic lockers are available for rent at Water World for a small daily fee.

Are children under a certain height admitted free?▾

Children under 100 cm in height typically receive complimentary park admission when accompanied by a paying adult.

Are rides safe and regularly inspected?▾

Yes, all roller coasters and aquatic facilities are certified according to international ISO safety standards and supervised by certified lifeguards.

Can adults enjoy the water park as well as children?▾

Absolutely. Siam Amazing Park features extreme thrill coasters in the X-Zone and giant speed slides alongside family-friendly splash areas and shaded beach loungers.
