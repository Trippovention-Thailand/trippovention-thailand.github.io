Experience Overview

Witness Bangkok from Thailand's most iconic architectural masterpiece

Visit the iconic King Power Mahanakhon SkyWalk and enjoy breathtaking panoramic views of Bangkok from one of Thailand's tallest buildings. Experience the thrilling Glass Tray on the 78th floor, offering a unique perspective of the city below. Admire spectacular daytime views of Bangkok's skyline, the Chao Phraya River, and famous city landmarks from the observation decks. A perfect experience for photography enthusiasts and anyone looking to witness Bangkok from above.

Key Experience Highlights

Unmatched sky-high perspectives and thrilling photo opportunities

Rooftop Thrill

The 78th-Floor Glass Tray

Step onto one of the world's largest glass tray floors suspended 310 meters above the city. Look straight down through transparent glass for an adrenaline-pumping perspective of Bangkok's bustling streets.

Observation Decks

360° Panoramic Views

Access both the indoor observation deck on the 74th floor and the outdoor rooftop deck on the 78th floor, capturing panoramic vistas of the Chao Phraya River, iconic temples, and endless skyscrapers.
