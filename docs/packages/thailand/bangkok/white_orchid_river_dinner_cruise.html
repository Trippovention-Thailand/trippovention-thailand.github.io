Tour Overview & Description

International & Seafood Buffet, Live Cabaret Show, 360° Open-Air Deck & Illuminated Sights

Spend an unforgettable evening aboard the White Orchid River Cruise, one of Bangkok's premier multi-deck dining vessels. Glide along the historic Chao Phraya River as the capital's iconic monuments—including Wat Arun, the Grand Palace, and Rama VIII Bridge—glow under night illumination.

Indulge in a lavish international and seafood buffet dinner accompanied by live pop music performances and an entertaining onboard classical and cabaret performance. The expansive 360-degree open-air top deck offers unmatched skyline views and refreshing evening river breezes.

Places Covered & Highlights

Experience the highlights and sights included on this journey

Onboard Luxury

Dining & Entertainment

  • International & Seafood Buffet: Generous selection of grilled river prawns, fresh sushi, Thai curries, Western dishes, and tropical desserts.
  • Live Cabaret & Thai Dance: Dazzling onboard performance featuring graceful classical Thai dance and vibrant cabaret entertainment.
  • Live Musical Duo: Talented live musicians performing romantic ballads and popular international hits throughout dinner.
Illuminated Views

Nighttime Landmarks

  • Illuminated Wat Arun: Gaze up at the glowing golden spire of the Temple of Dawn from the open river.
  • Grand Palace & Wat Phra Kaew: Magnificent royal walls and temple roofs illuminated in brilliant amber and gold light.
  • Rama VIII Cable Bridge: Spectacular single-tower cable-stayed bridge spanning the northern riverfront.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about this Bangkok activity

What is the difference between the White Orchid and Princess dinner cruises?▾

White Orchid is celebrated for its signature live cabaret performance in addition to live music, and features an expansive open-air top deck with 360-degree unobstructed views.

Is there vegetarian or Indian food available on the buffet?▾

Yes, the international buffet includes clearly labeled vegetarian options, fresh salads, pasta, and select Indian curries alongside Thai specialties and grilled seafood.

Where does the cruise depart from?▾

The White Orchid typically departs from Asiatique The Riverfront Pier. Specific boarding pier details and check-in desk numbers are confirmed on your travel voucher.

Can we request upper deck outdoor seating?▾

Yes, upper deck outdoor seating can be requested at the time of booking, subject to availability and fair weather conditions.
