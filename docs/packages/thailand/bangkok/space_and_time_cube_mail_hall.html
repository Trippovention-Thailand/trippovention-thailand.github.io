Exhibition Overview

Where cutting-edge technology blends with futuristic artistic imagination

Step into the immersive world of Space & Time Cube+, a one-of-a-kind digital art attraction located at Mail Hall in Bangkok. Explore a variety of interactive themed zones featuring mesmerizing light installations, mirror rooms, projection mapping, and cutting-edge visual effects. Walk through imaginative spaces designed for stunning photos and engaging experiences that blend art, technology, and creativity. Suitable for visitors of all ages, Space & Time Cube+ offers an unforgettable indoor adventure, making it a perfect destination for families, couples, friends, and photography enthusiasts.

Experience Highlights

Interactive zones crafted for wonder, creativity, and unforgettable photography

Visual Wonder

Kaleidoscopic Mirror Rooms

Immerse yourself in infinite reflections created by precision-aligned mirrors and dynamic LED neon lights that shift seamlessly to create mesmerizing dimensional spaces.

Cutting-Edge Tech

3D Projection Mapping

Interact with real-time digital projections that respond to your presence. A multi-sensory journey blending digital art, music, and optical illusions suitable for all generations.
