Attraction Overview

Bangkok's favorite open zoo and marine entertainment destination for all ages

Enjoy a visit to Safari World, Bangkok’s famous wildlife park featuring both Safari Park and Marine Park. Experience a thrilling drive-through safari where you can see animals like lions, tigers, and giraffes in open surroundings. Continue to Marine Park to enjoy exciting shows like dolphin, sea lion, and orangutan performances. A perfect blend of wildlife adventure and entertaining experiences for all age groups.

Two Parks, Endless Adventure

Explore the distinct zones of Safari World & Marine Park

Open Zoo

Safari Park Drive-Through

Drive through 8 kilometers of natural habitat where African and Asian wildlife roam freely. Encounter majestic lions, Bengal tigers, towering giraffes, zebras, rhinos, and diverse exotic bird species from the safety and comfort of your vehicle.

World-Class Entertainment

Marine Park & Live Shows

Watch captivating live animal presentations including the famous Dolphin Show, amusing Sea Lion Show, entertaining Orangutan Boxing, Bird Show, and the thrilling Hollywood Cowboy Stunt Show.
