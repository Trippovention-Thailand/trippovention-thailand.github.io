Tour Overview & Description

Hop-On Hop-Off Chao Phraya Tourist Boat, Riverside Landmarks & Historic Piers

Experience the bustling energy of the River of Kings aboard the open-deck Chao Phraya Sightseeing Boat. Cruise smoothly past iconic landmarks, century-old churches, royal temples, modern waterfront skyscrapers, and bustling merchant piers.

With an unlimited all-day hop-on hop-off boat pass, you can explore Bangkok at your own pace without traffic jams. Hop off at Tha Tien for Wat Pho, Wat Arun Pier for the Temple of Dawn, Tha Chang for the Grand Palace, or IconSiam for world-class waterfront retail.

Places Covered & Highlights

Experience the highlights and sights included on this journey

Cultural Stops

Historic Piers & Temples

  • Tha Maharaj / Tha Chang Pier: Direct access to Grand Palace, Temple of the Emerald Buddha, and riverside cafes.
  • Wat Arun Pier: Step straight off the boat into the grounds of the majestic Temple of Dawn.
  • Tha Tien Pier: Gateway to Wat Pho Reclining Buddha, rooftop bars, and local street markets.
Lifestyle Stops

Modern Riverfronts

  • IconSiam Pier: Award-winning luxury destination featuring the indoor SookSiam floating market and multimedia water fountain show.
  • Asiatique The Riverfront Pier: Lively evening boardwalk with dining, giant Ferris wheel, and night markets.
  • Phra Arthit Pier: Access to bohemian Banglamphu, historic fortresses, and Khao San Road.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about this Bangkok activity

How frequent are the sightseeing boats?▾

Chao Phraya Tourist Boats depart approximately every 30 minutes between Sathorn Pier and Phra Arthit Pier throughout the day.

Where do I collect my boat pass?▾

You can present your digital booking confirmation voucher at the ticket booth at Sathorn Central Pier (BTS Saphan Taksin) or Tha Maharaj Pier to collect your physical day pass.

Can I hop on and off as many times as I want?▾

Yes, the All-Day River Pass gives you unlimited rides across all 11 designated tourist piers until 06:30 PM.

Are boats wheelchair or stroller accessible?▾

Most modern catamarans on this route provide wide ramps and designated space for strollers and wheelchairs with crew assistance at major piers.
