Enchanting Chiang Mai Highland Escape

Experience the perfect blend of culture, nature, and adventure as you explore Chiang Mai's ancient temples, ethical elephant sanctuary, majestic mountains, cascading waterfalls, and vibrant café culture.

🗓️

Duration

5 Days / 4 Nights

Detailed Itinerary

Day 1

Arrival & Chiang Mai Heritage Tour

  • Arrive at Chiang Mai International Airport.
  • Meet & greet by our local representative.
  • Transfer to your hotel and check in.
  • Explore the historic Chiang Mai Old City.
  • Visit the city's ancient temples and charming streets.
  • Spend the evening exploring the vibrant Chiang Mai Night Bazaar.
Meals: None
Day 2

Elephant Sanctuary & Temple Experience

  • Breakfast at the hotel.
  • Visit the ethical Elephant Nature Park.
  • Continue to the iconic Wat Phra That Doi Suthep, offering spectacular views of Chiang Mai.
  • In the evening, unwind with a traditional Thai Spa Experience (optional).
Meals: Breakfast
Tickets: Included
Day 3

Doi Inthanon National Park Adventure

  • Breakfast at the hotel.
  • Discover the breathtaking Doi Inthanon National Park, Thailand's highest peak.
  • Visit the stunning Wachirathan Waterfall.
  • Explore the magnificent King & Queen Twin Royal Pagodas.
  • Experience the culture of the Karen Hill Tribe Village.
  • Return to the hotel in the evening.
Meals: Breakfast
Tickets: Included
Day 4

Local Flavours & Hidden Gems

  • Breakfast at the hotel.
  • Participate in an authentic Thai Cooking Class.
  • Visit the unique Sticky Waterfall (Bua Thong Waterfall).
  • Spend the afternoon café hopping around the trendy Nimmanhaemin Road.
  • Return to the hotel.
Meals: Breakfast
Tickets: Included
Day 5

Departure

  • Breakfast at the hotel.
  • Check out from the hotel.
  • Transfer to Chiang Mai International Airport.
  • Depart with unforgettable memories of Northern Thailand.
Meals: Breakfast
