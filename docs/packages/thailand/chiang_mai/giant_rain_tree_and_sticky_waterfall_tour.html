Tour Overview & Description

Experience two of Northern Thailand's most delightful natural phenomenon

Discover two of Chiang Mai's most unique natural attractions on this exhilarating guided tour. Begin with a visit to the iconic Giant Rain Tree, a magnificent centuries-old botanical wonder standing gracefully in the middle of a picturesque countryside landscape with an expansive, umbrella-like canopy that spans hundreds of feet across.

Continue to the world-famous Sticky Waterfall (Bua Tong Waterfall), where calcium carbonate mineral deposits have coated the limestone rocks with a unique traction surface, allowing you to walk and climb directly up the flowing multi-tiered waterfall without slipping! Enjoy scenic countryside vistas, a peaceful traditional riverboat ride, and a relaxing stop at The Garden's House before returning to Chiang Mai.

Places Covered

Natural attractions and scenic highlights featured on this excursion

Natural Wonders

Unique Botanical & Geological Sights

  • Giant Rain Tree: A massive centuries-old Albizia saman tree with a majestic, wide-spreading botanical canopy.
  • Sticky Waterfall (Bua Tong Waterfall): Multiple cascading levels of non-slip limestone mineral rock.
  • Nam Phu Chet Si (Seven Colors Spring): A pristine mineral spring creating azure pools that feed the waterfall.
  • Tropical Forest Trails: Shaded walking paths through dense northern jungle flora and fauna.
Leisure & River Excursion

Scenic Countryside & Waterways

  • Scenic Riverboat Ride: Glide peacefully along local northern waterways surrounded by tranquil rural scenery.
  • The Garden's House: Relaxing garden stopover for picturesque photography and countryside ambiance.
  • Natural Swimming Pools: Refreshing natural pools at the base of the waterfall perfect for cooling dips.
  • Scenic Country Roads: Comfortable van transit through northern agricultural plains and mountain valleys.
