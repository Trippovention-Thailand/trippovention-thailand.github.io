Tour Overview & Description

A compassionate, responsible elephant encounter in lush northern jungle foothills

Experience an ethical and heartwarming encounter with rescued Asian elephants at Kerchor Elephant Eco Park near Chiang Mai. Kerchor is dedicated to sustainable, cruelty-free conservation where no riding is permitted and elephants roam freely in an expansive, natural forest habitat.

Begin with an insightful briefing about Asian elephant behavior and welfare before preparing nutritious herbal and vitamin food balls. Hand-feed the gentle giants with sugar cane and bananas, walk alongside them along shaded jungle trails, and join them in a cool natural river for a fun bath and therapeutic mud spa. Conclude your adventure by savoring an authentic Thai local meal in open-air dining pavilions surrounded by breathtaking jungle greenery.

Places & Activities Covered

Hands-on conservation activities and natural habitats in Kerchor Eco Park

Conservation & Feeding

Park Zones & Interaction

  • Kerchor Elephant Eco Park Sanctuary: Protected rural highland sanctuary free from riding and performance hooks.
  • Elephant Nutrition & Feeding Area: Prepare organic dietary supplements and feed fresh bananas and sugarcane.
  • Herbal & Vitamin Ball Workshop: Hand-craft traditional digestive medicine balls using tamarind, rice, and herbs.
  • Forest Walking Trail: Walk peacefully beside the elephant herd through natural forested hillside pathways.
Bathing & Wellness

River Splash & Dining

  • Natural River Bathing: Splash and scrub the gentle giants in cool, flowing mountain streams.
  • Therapeutic Mud Spa: Coat the elephants in natural mineral mud to protect their skin from sun and insects.
  • Traditional Northern Lunch: Freshly prepared Thai local specialties, tropical fruit, and refreshments.
  • Shower & Changing Facilities: Clean facilities to wash up and change into dry clothing after the water activities.
