Tour Overview & Description

Experience Thailand's most visionary contemporary Buddhist temples and artistic wonders

Discover the cultural treasures and artistic marvels of Northern Thailand on this full-day guided excursion featuring the region's most iconic contemporary temples. Journey across scenic mountain passes, stopping first at the natural Mae Kachan Hot Springs to relax, soak your feet, or watch eggs boil in the thermal water.

Marvel at the breathtaking White Temple (Wat Rong Khun), created by renowned national artist Chalermchai Kositpipat, with its intricate alabaster bridge of rebirth and mirrored glass mosaics that shimmer in the sunlight. Next, admire the striking sapphire-and-gold architecture of the Blue Temple (Wat Rong Suea Ten) featuring magnificent naga dragons and a colossal white Buddha, followed by a visit to the whimsical fairy-tale mist garden at Lalitta Café. With comfortable transfers, a delicious local Thai lunch, and an expert guide, this tour offers the perfect harmony of contemporary art, spiritual heritage, and northern charm.

Places Covered

Iconic contemporary temples, thermal springs, and fairy-tale cafés

Masterwork Temples

Sacred Art & Architecture

  • Wat Rong Khun (White Temple): Masterpiece by Chalermchai Kositpipat featuring the bridge of rebirth and glistening mirror mosaics.
  • Wat Rong Suea Ten (Blue Temple): Vivid deep sapphire-blue interior with shimmering gold accents, dragon staircases, and pearl-white Buddha.
  • Contemporary Mural Galleries: Fascinating modern murals blending traditional Buddhist cosmology with pop culture allegories.
  • Art Gallery Pavilion: Displaying original paintings, sketches, and sculptures by Thailand's leading master artists.
Nature & Lifestyle

Thermal Springs & Mystical Gardens

  • Mae Kachan Hot Springs: Natural bubbling geothermal springs with relaxing foot-soaking mineral channels.
  • Lalitta Café: Enchanting rainforest garden cafe surrounded by mossy boulders, waterfalls, and ethereal artificial mist.
  • Mae Kachan Hill Tribe Village: Optional visit to meet local craftswomen and learn about indigenous Northern traditions.
  • Scenic Mountain Transit: Picturesque journey traversing the lush highlands connecting Chiang Mai and Chiang Rai provinces.
