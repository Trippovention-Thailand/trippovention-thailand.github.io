Tour Overview & Description

Ascend to the roof of Thailand amidst misty mountains, royal monuments & thundering falls

Explore the breathtaking natural beauty and cool mountain climate of Doi Inthanon National Park, home to Thailand's highest summit at 2,565 meters above sea level. This full-day guided excursion takes you through emerald highland landscapes, panoramic scenic viewpoints, and the tranquil mossy Ang Ka Nature Trail before visiting the iconic Twin Royal Pagodas, built to commemorate King Rama IX and Queen Sirikit.

Continue your highland journey with a culturally enriching stop at the local Mae Klang hill tribe market, where local Karen and Hmong vendors offer fresh mountain produce and handicrafts, and admire the majestic Wachirathan Waterfall cascading thunderously down granite cliffs. Perfect for nature enthusiasts, photographers, and families, this comprehensive tour showcases the very finest highland wonders of Northern Thailand.

Places Covered

Iconic highland landmarks and scenic wonders visited on this full-day tour

Summit & Royal Heritage

Highland Landmarks

  • Doi Inthanon National Park: Protected alpine biosphere featuring evergreen cloud forests and rare birdlife.
  • Thailand's Highest Point: Stand at the nation's summit (2,565m) and visit the memorial stupa of King Inthawichayanon.
  • Twin Royal Pagodas: Napamathanidol and Napaphonphumisiri stupas surrounded by immaculately sculpted flower gardens.
  • Ang Ka Nature Trail: A peaceful wooden boardwalk through a mystical, moss-draped prehistoric cloud forest.
Cascades & Culture

Waterfalls & Local Communities

  • Wachirathan Waterfall: A powerful, multi-tiered cataract plunging over steep granite rocks creating perpetual rainbows.
  • Mae Klang Hill Tribe Market: Vibrant community bazaar offering highland Arabica coffee, dried fruit, and local textiles.
  • Scenic Mountain Drives: Panoramic winding roads through forested ridges and deep valleys of the Thanon Thong Chai Range.
  • Flora & Alpine Gardens: Cool-climate flowers, exotic rhododendrons, and lush Himalayan temperate flora.
