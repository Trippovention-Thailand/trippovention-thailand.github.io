Experience Overview

A compassionate, responsible, and heartwarming connection with rescued gentle giants

Experience an unforgettable ethical elephant encounter at the Elephant Sanctuary, where rescued elephants live in a safe and natural environment. Learn about elephant conservation, their behavior, and the sanctuary's commitment to responsible tourism. Feed the elephants, walk alongside them through the jungle, enjoy a fun mud spa, and bathe them in a natural setting. You'll also learn about traditional rubber tree cultivation and participate in a Pad Thai cooking session. This half-day experience offers the perfect blend of wildlife, culture, and adventure, making it ideal for families, couples, and nature enthusiasts.

Program Highlights & Activities

Hands-on ethical care and cultural adventures in the lush Phuket rainforest

Ethical Care

Feeding & Natural Interaction

Prepare nutritious food baskets of bananas, sugar cane, and dietary supplements. Feed the elephants directly by hand and observe their playful social behaviors under the watchful supervision of ethical mahouts.

Jungle Exploration

Jungle Trail & Rubber Plantation

Accompany the elephants on a gentle scenic walk along forested jungle trails. Along the route, learn about indigenous rubber tree tapping and the traditional agricultural heritage of southern Thailand.

Spa & Bathing

Mud Spa & Natural Bath

Join the elephants for a revitalizing mud spa treatment that protects their sensitive skin from sun and insects, followed by splashing and scrubbing them clean in a cool natural freshwater stream.

Culinary Workshop

Pad Thai Cooking Session

Conclude your wildlife morning with an interactive cooking demonstration. Master the secrets of authentic Pad Thai noodles using fresh local herbs and ingredients, and enjoy your freshly made culinary creation.
