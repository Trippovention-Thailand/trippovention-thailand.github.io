Tour Overview

Witness dramatic karst pinnacles, mystical sea caves, and cinematic Andaman landscapes

Embark on an exciting full-day speedboat adventure to the world-famous James Bond Island in Phang Nga Bay, renowned for its dramatic limestone cliffs and emerald-green waters. Cruise across the Andaman Sea by speedboat and explore the breathtaking landscapes that gained international fame from The Man with the Golden Gun. Enjoy an unforgettable sea canoeing experience through hidden caves, mangrove forests, and tranquil lagoons while admiring the region's natural beauty. Relax on pristine beaches, savor a delicious Thai lunch, and take in the spectacular scenery throughout the day. This tour is ideal for families, couples, friends, and nature lovers seeking adventure and stunning coastal landscapes.

Key Itinerary Highlights

Experience the very best highlights of Phang Nga Bay Marine National Park

007 Legend

Khao Phing Kan & Ko Tapu

Walk the famous shores of Khao Phing Kan and capture unforgettable photos in front of Ko Tapu, the 20-meter sheer limestone needle rising from emerald waters made legendary by the James Bond film.

Sea Canoeing

Panak & Hong Island Caves

Board inflatable canoes piloted by expert local paddlers into the dark stalactite caverns and concealed sea chambers (hongs) of Panak and Hong Islands, opening into tranquil secret lagoons.

Cultural Stop

Koh Panyee Floating Village

Cruise past the centuries-old Muslim community of Koh Panyee, an entire village built on stilts against a towering vertical rock cliff in the center of the bay, complete with its famous floating football pitch.

Island Relaxation

Beach Leisure & Thai Buffet Lunch

Savor a flavorful Thai buffet lunch prepared fresh for the tour, and unwind with sunbathing, swimming, and beach photography on soft sand shores before heading back across Phang Nga Bay.
