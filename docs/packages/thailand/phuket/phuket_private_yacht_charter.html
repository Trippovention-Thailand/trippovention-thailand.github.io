Tour Overview & Description

Exclusive VIP Island Hopping to Maiton, Coral, or Racha Islands with Private Crew & Captain

Elevate your Thailand vacation to the height of luxury with an exclusive private yacht or catamaran charter across the turquoise waters of the Andaman Sea. Tailored exclusively for discerning families, corporate VIPs, and celebratory gatherings, a private yacht charter offers complete privacy, flexibility, and five-star hospitality.

Cruise at your own pace to secluded tropical archipelagos—including Maiton Island's resident dolphin grounds, Racha Yai's crystal bays, or the dramatic limestone pillars of Phang Nga Bay. With a dedicated licensed captain, attentive private crew, gourmet catering options, and water sports toys, your day on the water is completely bespoke.

Places Covered & Highlights

Experience the highlights and sights included on this journey

VIP Itineraries

Custom Island Routes

  • Maiton Island Dolphin Quest & Snorkel: Cruise to private Maiton Island, renowned for its wild pod of playful Indo-Pacific bottlenose dolphins and pristine shallow coral reefs.
  • Racha Yai & Coral Island Combo: Anchor in turquoise lagoons with 25-meter underwater clarity, secluded white sandy beaches, and vibrant marine life.
  • Phang Nga Bay & Hong Island Caves: Sail between towering 300-meter karst pinnacles with private sea kayaking into hidden limestone hongs.
Five-Star Comfort

Onboard Luxury Amenities

  • Air-Conditioned Saloon & Sun Deck: Expansive interior living lounge, state-of-the-art Bluetooth sound system, outdoor flybridge, and cushioned sun loungers.
  • Water Sports & Toys: Complimentary use of stand-up paddleboards, inflatable sea pools, high-end snorkeling gear, and fishing lines.
  • Gourmet Catering & Refreshments: Optional freshly prepared seafood BBQ, gourmet fruit platters, soft drinks, and ice; bring your own wine with zero corkage fees.
