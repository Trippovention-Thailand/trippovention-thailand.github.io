Tour Overview & Description

Turquoise Waters of Koh Hey, Banana Beach Water Sports & Racha Yai Coral Snorkeling

Escape to two of Phuket's most beautiful neighboring islands on a scenic speedboat day cruise to Coral Island (Koh Hey) and Racha Yai Island (Koh Racha). Located just 9 and 20 kilometers south of Phuket respectively, these islands offer tranquil tropical shores, crystal-clear visibility, and powdery white sand beaches.

Spend your morning at Coral Island's vibrant Banana Beach, relaxing under swaying palm trees or engaging in optional thrilling water sports such as parasailing, sea walking, or banana boat rides. In the afternoon, cruise to Racha Yai to snorkel in translucent aquamarine bays surrounded by colorful parrotfish, triggerfish, and living coral reefs.

Places Covered & Highlights

Experience the highlights and sights included on this journey

Beach & Water Sports

Coral Island (Koh Hey)

  • Banana Beach / Long Beach: Stunning curve of white sand fringed by jungle hills, perfect for sunbathing, swimming, and beach photography.
  • Optional Thrill Water Sports: Participate in thrilling parasailing over the bay, sea walking along the seabed, or banana boat rides.
  • Shallow Reef Snorkeling: Snorkel directly off the beach among schools of butterflyfish and vibrant sea urchins.
Aquamarine Snorkeling

Racha Yai Island (Koh Racha)

  • Patok Bay & Siam Bay: Horseshoe-shaped sheltered bays boasting crystal-clear underwater visibility up to 25 meters deep.
  • Deep Coral Reef Snorkel Stop: Open-water boat snorkeling session guided by marine instructors over healthy coral garden heads.
  • Island Buffet Lunch: Relish a hearty Thai buffet lunch served at a cliffside or beachside island restaurant.
