Experience Overview

A legendary nighttime extravaganza blending ancient Siamese myths with cutting-edge theatrical arts

Experience an unforgettable evening at Phuket FantaSea, Thailand's iconic cultural theme park that blends the country's rich heritage with world-class entertainment. Explore the lively Festival Village featuring traditional handicrafts, shopping outlets, games, and cultural exhibits before indulging in a lavish international buffet dinner at the Golden Kinnaree Restaurant. The highlight of the evening is the spectacular Phuket FantaSea Show, a 70-minute theatrical production featuring breathtaking acrobatics, dazzling special effects, elaborate costumes, stunning stage performances, and captivating storytelling inspired by Thai mythology and culture. Perfect for families, couples, and travelers of all ages, this award-winning attraction promises a magical night filled with entertainment, culture, and unforgettable memories.

Theme Park Attractions

Immerse yourself in mythical Thai wonderlands across the Kamala grounds

Grand Theater

Palace of the Elephants

A magnificent 3,000-seat theater modeled after a mythical Sukhothai-era stone palace. Witness "Fantasy of a Kingdom", a mesmerizing 70-minute show featuring illusionists, aerial acrobatics, pyrotechnics, and cultural folklore.

Royal Dining

Golden Kinnaree Buffet

Dine in one of the world's largest banquet halls, flanked by golden mythical Kinnaree statues and a lotus lake. Enjoy an expansive international buffet with Thai royal recipes, Indian vegetarian curries, Western classics, and desserts.

Shopping & Crafts

Festival Village

Stroll through an enchanted village showcasing Thai architectural styles from across the country. Enjoy cultural carnival parades, live craft demonstrations like silk weaving and pottery, and unique boutique shopping.

Wild Safari

Similan Adventure Center

Take an interactive walk-through safari inspired by Thailand's mythical underwater world. Encounter exotic tropical birds, unique flora, and glowing neon animal exhibits designed for explorers of all ages.
