Experience Overview

Step into a breathtaking realm of light, color, Thai festivities, and world-class entertainment

Experience the enchanting world of Carnival Magic, Phuket's award-winning nighttime theme park that combines Thai culture, dazzling entertainment, and spectacular carnival-themed attractions. Stroll through vibrant streets filled with colorful lights, unique shopping outlets, and interactive displays before indulging in a delicious international buffet dinner. The highlight of the evening is the world-famous Carnival Magic Parade Show, featuring magnificent parade floats, stunning costumes, cutting-edge lighting effects, and captivating performances inspired by Thailand's rich cultural heritage. This magical experience is perfect for families, couples, and travelers of all ages looking for an unforgettable evening in Phuket.

Spectacular Theme Park Zones

Explore the dazzling wonderlands inside Carnival Magic Phuket

Parade Stadium

River Carnival Parade Show

Held inside the majestic River Palace stadium, this world-record-breaking indoor theatrical parade features colossal floats, hundreds of talented performers, aerial dancers, and dazzling animatronics celebrating Thailand's legendary festivals.

Light Kingdom

Kingdom of Lights

Wander through an awe-inspiring illumination park decorated with over 40 million shimmering LED lights. Marvel at glowing swan lakes, enchanted tropical gardens, and towering mythical sculptures creating infinite photo opportunities.

Grand Dining

Bird of Paradise Buffet Restaurant

Dine in a palace-like banquet hall surrounded by mythical peacocks. Savor a lavish international buffet with authentic Thai delicacies, fresh seafood, Western favorites, vegetarian selections, and delectable desserts.

Cultural Carnival

Carnival Fun Fair

Explore vibrant streets celebrating traditional Thai temple fairs, filled with interactive arcade games, street performers, artisanal handicrafts, souvenir boutique shops, and festive carnival snack stalls.
