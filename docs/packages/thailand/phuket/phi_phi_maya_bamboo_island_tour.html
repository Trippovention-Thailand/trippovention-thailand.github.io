Tour Overview & Description

Maya Bay, Pileh Lagoon, Viking Cave, Monkey Beach, and Pristine Bamboo Island Snorkeling

Embark on the ultimate Andaman Sea voyage to the world-famous Phi Phi archipelago aboard a modern, twin-engine speedboat. Renowned worldwide for its soaring limestone cliffs, electric-blue lagoons, and powder-soft white sand beaches, Phi Phi stands as Thailand's crowning tropical jewel.

Walk the iconic powdery white sands of Maya Bay—the setting for 'The Beach'—swim in the emerald waters of enclosed Pileh Lagoon, photograph ancient paintings at Viking Cave, observe macaques at Monkey Beach, and unwind on the secluded coral shores of uninhabited Bamboo Island (Koh Mai Phai) with an authentic beach buffet lunch.

Places Covered & Highlights

Experience the highlights and sights included on this journey

Limestone Marvels

Phi Phi Leh Highlights

  • Maya Bay: World-renowned enclosed bay framed by 100-meter limestone karsts with pristine coral restoration reefs and baby blacktip reef sharks.
  • Pileh Lagoon (Emerald Pool): A breathtaking natural lagoon carved into limestone cliffs, offering calm, turquoise waters perfect for jumping and floating.
  • Viking Cave & Monkey Beach: Historic sea cavern where swiftlet birds' nests are harvested, followed by close-up views of wild long-tailed macaques on Monkey Beach.
Snorkeling & Relaxation

Bamboo Island & Phi Phi Don

  • Bamboo Island (Koh Mai Phai): A picture-postcard tropical island encircled by a ring of pure white coral sand and shallow turquoise reef flats.
  • Phi Phi Don Island Buffet Lunch: Delicious Thai and international buffet lunch served at a beachside restaurant overlooking Ton Sai Bay.
  • Shark Point & Coral Snorkel Stops: Two guided open-water snorkeling sessions among schools of sergeant majors, parrotfish, and sea anemones.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about this Phuket activity

To protect the delicate marine ecosystem and resident blacktip reef sharks, wading into the water at Maya Bay is permitted up to knee height, but full swimming is strictly prohibited by national park authorities. Plenty of deep-water swimming and snorkeling take place at Pileh Lagoon and Bamboo Island.

Speedboat travel across open sea can be bumpy depending on wind and wave conditions. For safety reasons, maritime regulations prohibit pregnant women and passengers with severe back, neck, or spinal problems from joining open-sea speedboat tours.

National Park entrance fees for the Hat Noppharat Thara-Mu Ko Phi Phi National Park (THB 400 per adult, THB 200 per child) are set by the Thai Department of National Parks and are collected in cash at the marina reception before boarding.

Passenger safety is our top operational priority. If the Marine Department issues weather warnings or wave heights exceed safety limits, tours are either rescheduled to a calmer day or rerouted to sheltered bays, or fully refunded.
