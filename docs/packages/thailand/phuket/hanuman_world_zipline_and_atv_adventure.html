Tour Overview & Description

Canopy Ziplining, Sky Walk Bridge, Roller Zipline & Rugged Jungle ATV Expedition

Soar high above the rainforest canopy and conquer rugged jungle tracks with Phuket's premier eco-adventure at Hanuman World. Set within 100,000 square meters of lush tropical hillside jungle in Wichit, this state-of-the-art outdoor park combines exhilarating aerial challenges with raw off-road motorized excitement.

Glide between giant ancient banyan trees across 16 or 30 treetop platforms, spiral down abseil points, conquer sky bridges, and brave the 800-meter-long Roller Zipline—a high-speed twisting gravity coaster through the trees. Follow up with an adrenaline-pumping ATV quad bike trail traversing mud trails, river crossings, and hillside ridges.

Places Covered & Highlights

Experience the highlights and sights included on this journey

Aerial Adventure

Canopy & Roller Zipline Thrills

  • Treetop Zipline Course: Fly across up to 30 jungle platforms connected by high-speed dual-wire cable ziplines reaching lengths of up to 400 meters.
  • Twisting Roller Zipline: An 800-meter roller coaster zipline curving, diving, and swooping through the forest branches at up to 40 km/h.
  • Sky Walk Treetop Bridge: Elevated timber canopy walkway offering 360-degree panoramic rainforest views and educational insights on tropical flora.
Off-Road Action

All-Terrain Vehicle (ATV) Safari

  • Jungle Trail ATV Track: Guide-led quad bike expedition navigating gravel tracks, natural forest obstacles, mud pits, and steep hill climbs.
  • Panoramic Ridge Stops: Scenic jungle viewpoint halts overlooking Phuket's forested interior with photography breaks.
  • Safety Briefing & Training Track: Introductory practice circuit to master quad bike steering, throttle control, and braking before tackling the wild trails.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about this Phuket activity

Participants must be at least 4 years old. The maximum weight limit for zipline and roller zipline activities is 120 kg (264 lbs). Pregnant women and individuals with heart conditions or spinal injuries cannot participate.

No formal driver's license is required. Operators must be at least 15 years old to drive an ATV independently. Children aged 4–14 can ride as passengers behind an adult rider or instructor on double quad bikes.

Sturdy closed-toe athletic sneakers or hiking shoes are mandatory. Open-toe sandals, flip-flops, crocs, and high heels are strictly forbidden on ziplines and ATVs for safety reasons.
