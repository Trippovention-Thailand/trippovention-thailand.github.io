Tour Overview & Description

100% Flexible VIP Itinerary with Private Air-Conditioned Van & Dedicated Local Chauffeur

Experience the ultimate freedom and comfort of exploring Phuket on a 100% customizable private sightseeing tour. Ideal for families with children, senior travelers, romantic couples, and small private groups, this tour gives you complete control over your sightseeing schedule without the rush of join-in tour groups.

Travel in a modern, spacious air-conditioned minivan driven by an experienced, courteous local chauffeur. Choose your own stops from Phuket's finest sights—including the Big Buddha, Wat Chalong, Promthep Cape, Windmill Viewpoint, Karon Viewpoint, Phuket Old Town cafes, or seaside seafood restaurants in Rawai—and explore each destination at your own relaxed pace.

Places Covered & Highlights

Experience the highlights and sights included on this journey

Tailored Culture

Popular Cultural Stops

  • Big Buddha of Phuket: Marvel at the 45-meter marble monument on Nakkerd Hill with panoramic 360-degree views across the southern island.
  • Wat Chalong Royal Temple: Explore the historic Grand Pagoda, admire golden statues, and experience traditional Thai Buddhist culture.
  • Phuket Old Town Sino-Portuguese Lanes: Stroll boutique cafes, art galleries, and historic shophouses along Thalang Road and Soi Romanee.
Scenic Panoramas

Breathtaking Coastal Viewpoints

  • Promthep Cape & Windmill Lookout: Phuket's legendary sunset cape and adjacent windmill plateau overlooking Nai Harn and Ya Nui beaches.
  • Karon Viewpoint (Three Beaches Vista): Spectacular coastal overlook framing Kata Noi, Kata, and Karon bays.
  • Rawai Seafood Market & Pier: Select live Andaman lobster, prawns, and fish at the gypsy market and have it cooked fresh at seaside eateries.
