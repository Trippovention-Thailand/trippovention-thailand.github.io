Tour Overview & Description

100+ Performers, Flying Angels, Live River on Stage & Traditional Thai Village Experience

Immerse yourself in Thailand's rich history, spiritual traditions, and vibrant cultural celebrations at Siam Niramit Phuket. Billed as one of the world's largest stage productions, this monumental theatrical extravaganza brings 700 years of Siamese heritage to life across a gigantic 65-meter-wide stage in a 1,750-seat state-of-the-art auditorium.

Before the main curtain rises, explore the charming Traditional Thai Village—a living museum where artisans demonstrate silk weaving, rice pounding, and traditional cooking in authentic wooden houses representing Thailand's four distinct regions. Enjoy a lavish international buffet dinner and outdoor fountain performances before being captivated by airborne angels, thunderous battle scenes, and a real river flowing across the stage.

Places Covered & Highlights

Experience the highlights and sights included on this journey

Epic Production

Three Theatrical Acts

  • Act 1: Journey Back into History: Spectacular reenactments of ancient Siam: the ancient northern kingdom of Lanna, South Sea maritime traders, and Ayutthaya royal court.
  • Act 2: Journey Beyond Imagination: Visually stunning depiction of the three Buddhist realms: fiery Hell, mystical Himmapan Forest with mythical creatures, and Blissful Heaven.
  • Act 3: Journey Through Joyous Festivals: Joyous celebration of Thailand's greatest festive traditions: floating lanterns during Loy Krathong and water splashing during Songkran.
Living Heritage

Cultural Village & Outdoor Pre-Show

  • Traditional Thai Village of the Four Regions: Wander through authentic stilt houses depicting Northern, Central, Southern, and Isan rural lifestyles with artisan workshops.
  • Outdoor Courtyard Entertainment: Enjoy live Thai boxing (Muay Thai) demonstrations, classical live gamelan music, and traditional folk dances in the outdoor plaza.
  • Grand International Buffet Dinner: Feast on a vast selection of freshly prepared Thai favorites, international roast carving stations, vegetarian dishes, and desserts.
