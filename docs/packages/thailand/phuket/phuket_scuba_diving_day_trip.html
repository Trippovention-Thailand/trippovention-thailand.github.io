Tour Overview & Description

Certified & Discover Scuba Diving with PADI Instructors, Underwater Statues & Coral Reefs

Delve beneath the crystal-clear waters of the Andaman Sea with a premier scuba diving day trip to Racha Yai Island. Situated 20 kilometers south of Phuket, Racha Yai is internationally renowned for its calm, sheltered bays, exceptional underwater visibility ranging from 15 to 30 meters, and diverse marine life.

Whether you are a certified diver looking to explore submerged motorbikes and concrete elephant statues at Siam Bay, or a complete beginner eager to experience Discover Scuba Diving for the first time, our professional PADI instructors provide safe, small-ratio guided dives. Enjoy a spacious 3-deck dive boat equipped with sundeck, dive platform, and freshly cooked buffet meals.

Places Covered & Highlights

Experience the highlights and sights included on this journey

Submerged Wonders

Racha Yai Dive Sites

  • Siam Bay & Underwater Sculptures: Explore submerged concrete elephant statues, giant oyster shells, and temple gate replicas covered in soft corals.
  • Bungalow Bay Reefs: Sheltered bay with a gentle sloping sandy bottom, hard coral pinnacles, moray eels, and schools of yellowtail barracuda.
  • Homerock & Lucy's Reef: Vibrant drift dive site adorned with gorgonian sea fans, anemone clownfish colonies, and occasional passing manta rays.
First-Class Service

Vessel Comfort & PADI Standards

  • Spacious 3-Deck Custom Dive Vessel: Large dive deck, freshwater rinse showers, air-conditioned saloon, and rooftop padded sun terrace.
  • PADI Certified Instruction: Small diver ratios (maximum 4 certified divers per divemaster; 2 beginners per instructor) for personalized safety.
  • Freshly Cooked Onboard Meals: Hot breakfast served during the outward cruise, followed by a delicious freshly prepared Thai buffet lunch.
