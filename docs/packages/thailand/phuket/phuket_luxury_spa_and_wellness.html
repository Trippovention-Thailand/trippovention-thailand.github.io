Tour Overview & Description

Traditional Royal Thai Massage, Herbal Compress, Aromatherapy Oil & Floral Foot Bath

Surrender to blissful tranquility and restore total physical harmony with an award-winning luxury spa experience in Phuket. Set amidst serene tropical botanical gardens, trickling lotus ponds, and teakwood treatment pavilions, our partner wellness sanctuaries represent the pinnacle of traditional Siamese healing and holistic wellness.

Begin your journey with an invigorating kaffir lime and sea salt floral foot ritual, followed by your choice of therapeutic treatments: ancient Royal Thai acupressure massage, steaming organic herbal compresses (Luk Pra Kob), warm coconut oil body therapy, or exfoliating tropical body scrubs. Conclude with warm herbal tea and seasonal fruit in a peaceful relaxation lounge.

Places Covered & Highlights

Experience the highlights and sights included on this journey

Holistic Healing

Signature Treatment Rituals

  • Traditional Royal Thai Massage (Nuad Thai): Centuries-old therapeutic healing art combining acupressure, rhythmic muscle compression, and passive yoga stretching.
  • Hot Herbal Ball Compress (Luk Pra Kob): Steamed muslin bundles of turmeric, lemongrass, camphor, and plai applied along meridian lines to relieve muscle tension.
  • Aromatherapy Essential Oil Therapy: Gentle Swedish-inspired flowing massage using customized pure essential oils (jasmine, lemongrass, or lavender).
Botanical Serenity

Spa Sanctuary Facilities

  • Private Teakwood Treatment Villas: Air-conditioned private suites with terrazzo bathtubs, private rain showers, and tranquil garden views.
  • Floral Foot Cleansing Ritual: Soothing foot bath infused with fresh kaffir lime slices, sea salts, and fragrant orchid petals.
  • Post-Treatment Herbal Tea Lounge: Unwind after your therapy with hot organic bael fruit tea, herbal infusions, and fresh tropical fruit.
