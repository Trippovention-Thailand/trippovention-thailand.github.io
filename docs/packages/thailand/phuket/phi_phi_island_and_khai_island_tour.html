Tour Overview

Experience Thailand's most celebrated archipelago of sheer cliffs, turquoise lagoons & vibrant marine life

Embark on a full-day island-hopping adventure to the stunning Phi Phi Islands and Khai Island, two of Phuket's most beautiful tropical destinations. Cruise across the crystal-clear waters of the Andaman Sea and admire breathtaking limestone cliffs, turquoise lagoons, and pristine white-sand beaches. Enjoy leisure time for swimming, snorkeling, sunbathing, or simply relaxing amidst the spectacular scenery. Visit the picturesque Khai Island, renowned for its clear waters and vibrant marine life, making it an ideal spot for beach lovers and snorkeling enthusiasts. A delicious lunch is included, ensuring a memorable and enjoyable island experience for families, couples, and groups alike.

Island-Hopping Highlights

Discover the legendary bays and coral shallows of the Andaman Sea

Maya Bay & Lagoons

Phi Phi Leh Sightseeing

Cruise past legendary Maya Bay (featured in The Beach), swim in the emerald waters of Pileh Lagoon enclosed by towering 100-meter karst cliffs, and view the historic Viking Cave with its swiftlet bird nests.

Marine Life

Guided Coral Reef Snorkeling

Don your provided mask and snorkel to immerse yourself in thriving tropical coral gardens. Swim alongside schools of colorful clownfish, parrotfish, sergeant majors, and vibrant sea anemones.

Buffet Dining

Phi Phi Don Beachside Lunch

Anchor at Phi Phi Don, the largest island, to enjoy a wholesome international and Thai buffet lunch. Savor fresh stir-fries, curries, crispy chicken, and seasonal fruits with time to stroll the charming seaside promenade.

White Sand Shores

Khai Island Beach Relaxation

Stop at picturesque Khai Island, celebrated for its powder-soft white sand and shallow, child-friendly turquoise waters where tame tropical fish swim right up to the shoreline. Perfect for sunbathing and cooling off.
