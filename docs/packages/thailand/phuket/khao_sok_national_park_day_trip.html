Tour Overview & Description

Ancient Rainforest, Longtail Boat Cruise through Guilin Limestone Karsts & Floating Raft Lunch

Journey inland from Phuket into the primeval wilderness of Khao Sok National Park, home to one of the oldest evergreen rainforests on Earth—dating back over 160 million years. This epic full-day eco-adventure takes you to Cheow Lan Lake, an immense 165-square-kilometer emerald freshwater lake ringed by towering limestone peaks rising sheer from the mist.

Board a classic traditional wooden longtail boat at Ratchaprapha Dam to cruise through the awe-inspiring karst scenery known as Thailand's 'Little Guilin'. Marvel at the iconic Khao Sam Kler three-limestone pinnacles, enjoy a hearty southern Thai buffet lunch at a floating bamboo raft house, kayak on the calm emerald lake, and swim in pure freshwater surrounded by untouched nature.

Places Covered & Highlights

Experience the highlights and sights included on this journey

Limestone Karsts

Cheow Lan Lake Highlights

  • Ratchaprapha Dam & Lake Cruise: Scenic longtail boat cruise cutting through mirror-calm emerald waters between 500-meter vertical limestone cliffs.
  • Khao Sam Kler (Three Brothers): The signature photographic landmark of Cheow Lan Lake featuring three jagged limestone pinnacles rising dramatically from the depths.
  • Floating Raft House Relaxation: Step onto a traditional floating raft house community on the lake for lunch, relaxation, and scenic mountain views.
Freshwater Wilderness

Eco-Adventures & Swimming

  • Freshwater Lake Swimming & Kayaking: Dive directly into warm, crystalline freshwater and paddle self-guided kayaks around peaceful jungle bays.
  • Khao Sok Viewpoint Halt: Panoramic morning viewpoint halt overlooking the vast forested mountain ranges of Surat Thani province.
  • Rainforest Flora & Wildlife Observation: Spot hornbills, gibbons, wild langurs, and kingfishers inhabiting the lakeside jungle canopy.
