Tour Overview & Description

ISA-Certified Surf Lessons on Kata Beach & Scenic Flat-Water Stand-Up Paddleboarding

Catch your first wave or glide peacefully across tranquil tropical lagoons with Phuket's top-rated surfing and Stand-Up Paddleboard (SUP) experiences. With warm tropical water year-round and gentle, forgiving beach breaks, Phuket is recognized as Thailand's surf capital.

During the southwest monsoon swell season (May to October), Kata Beach transforms into a surfing haven perfect for beginners and intermediate riders under the guidance of ISA-certified instructors. During the calm dry season (November to April), take advantage of mirror-flat coastal bays and serene mangrove waterways for scenic stand-up paddleboarding excursions.

Places Covered & Highlights

Experience the highlights and sights included on this journey

Catch the Waves

Surf Lessons & Technique

  • Beachfront Theory & Pop-Up Drills: Learn ocean safety, wave reading, board positioning, paddling power, and correct pop-up footwork on the sand.
  • In-Water Guided Wave Catching: Your coach stays by your side in the water, pushing you into ideal waves and providing real-time stance adjustments.
  • Soft-Top Safe Beginner Boards: Ride high-volume foam soft-top surfboards that maximize stability, floatation, and safety for beginners.
Coastal Gliding

Stand-Up Paddleboard (SUP)

  • Scenic Bay & Island Paddling: Paddle along the picturesque coastline of Kata, Yanui, or Bang Tao, admiring coastal cliffs and marine life beneath your board.
  • Core Balance & Fitness: Enjoy an invigorating low-impact full-body workout improving balance, core strength, and mindfulness on the ocean.
  • Sunset Paddle Sessions: Experience magical golden hour paddling as the tropical sun dips below the Andaman horizon.
