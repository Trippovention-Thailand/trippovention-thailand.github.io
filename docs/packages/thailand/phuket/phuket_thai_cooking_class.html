Tour Overview & Description

Chef-Guided Fresh Market Ingredient Selection & Hands-On 4-Course Thai Culinary Workshop

Uncover the secrets of Thailand's internationally acclaimed gastronomy with an interactive, hands-on cooking masterclass in Phuket. Beyond learning recipes, this immersive culinary journey teaches you how to balance the fundamental flavor pillars of Thai food: sweet, sour, salty, spicy, and creamy.

Begin with a guided morning visit to a bustling local produce market to touch, smell, and select fresh galangal, kaffir lime leaves, lemongrass, and Thai chilies. At your individual cooking station, pound fresh curry paste from scratch with a stone mortar, cook four classic Thai dishes under expert chef supervision, and feast upon your own delicious creations.

Places Covered & Highlights

Experience the highlights and sights included on this journey

Hands-On Cooking

Market Tour & Master Skills

  • Local Morning Produce Market: Guided exploration of a local fresh market, learning to identify exotic herbs, tropical produce, and freshly pressed coconut cream.
  • Handmade Curry Paste from Scratch: Pound fresh shallots, garlic, dried red chilies, coriander root, and lemongrass in a traditional granite mortar and pestle.
  • Individual Cooking Workstations: Fully equipped prep counter and dedicated gas wok burner with personalized step-by-step guidance.
Signature Dishes

4-Course Menu Highlights

  • Tom Yum Goong / Tom Kha Gai: Iconic hot and sour prawn soup infused with lemongrass and lime, or velvety coconut chicken galangal soup.
  • Pad Thai Goong / Green Chicken Curry: Stir-fried rice noodles with tamarind sauce and tiger prawns, or fragrant Green Curry (Gaeng Keow Wan).
  • Mango Sticky Rice (Khao Niew Mamuang): Sweet warm coconut sticky rice served with ripe golden honey mangoes and toasted mung beans.
