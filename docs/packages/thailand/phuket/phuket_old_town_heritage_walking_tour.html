Tour Overview & Description

Sino-Portuguese Architecture on Thalang Road, Soi Romanee Murals & UNESCO Gastronomy Tastings

Step back into the 19th-century tin-mining boom era of Phuket with a captivating heritage and street food walking tour through Phuket Old Town. Recognized as a UNESCO City of Gastronomy, Phuket Town boasts a rich cultural tapestry where Chinese tin miners, Siamese locals, and Malay Peranakan merchants created a unique architectural and culinary legacy.

Wander through historic Thalang Road, Dibuk Road, and the colorful pastel lanes of Soi Romanee lined with ornate Sino-Portuguese shophouses and whimsical street murals. Along the way, visit incense-scented Chinese Taoist shrines, traditional herbal apothecaries, and savor authentic local delicacies like Hokkien noodles, crispy roti pancakes, and traditional Peranakan coconut pastries.

Places Covered & Highlights

Experience the highlights and sights included on this journey

Living History

Sino-Portuguese Architectural Gems

  • Thalang Road & Soi Romanee: Admire the ornate archways, shuttered wooden windows, and pastel Sino-colonial facades of restored 19th-century merchant houses.
  • San Chao Seng Tham (Shrine of the Serene Light): A hidden 130-year-old Chinese Taoist shrine tucked down a narrow alleyway adorned with intricate dragon sculptures and frescoes.
  • Phuket Heritage Street Murals: Discover whimsical urban wall murals depicting traditional tin miners, local food vendors, and historic street life.
Local Flavors

UNESCO Gastronomy Tastings

  • Hokkien Fried Noodles (Mee Hokkien): Savor rich stir-fried yellow egg noodles with pork, squid, and greens bathed in a savory seafood broth with a poached egg.
  • Phuket Spring Rolls & Dim Sum: Taste freshly rolled Popiah (fresh spring rolls with sweet bean sauce) and traditional Phuket-style morning dim sum.
  • O-Aew Shaved Ice Dessert: Cool down with Phuket's signature refreshing dessert made from jellied O-aew fig seeds, red beans, shaved ice, and sweet syrup.
