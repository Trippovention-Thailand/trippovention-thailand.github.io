Tour Overview & Description

World-Famous Transgender Cultural Music, Dance & Extravagant Costume Spectacular in Patong

Since opening its doors in 1991, Phuket Simon Cabaret has established itself as one of Southeast Asia's most iconic and celebrated theatrical entertainment shows. Housed in an intimate, air-conditioned 600-seat theatre on Sirirat Road in South Patong, the show delivers a dazzling, family-friendly evening of glamour, comedy, and high-energy showmanship.

Featuring an exceptional cast of talented transgender performers adorned in feathered headdresses, sparkling crystal ballgowns, and elaborate cultural regalia, the show takes audiences on a whirlwind musical journey spanning Hollywood Broadway, Bollywood extravaganzas, traditional Thai folklore, Chinese operatics, and modern pop anthems.

World Music & Dance

Theatrical & Musical Acts

  • Broadway & Hollywood Divas: Show-stopping tributes to international divas with synchronized choreography and shimmering sequined gowns.
  • Carnival & Latin Rhythms: High-tempo Rio-style carnival acts with cascading plumes of colorful ostrich feathers and infectious Latin beats.
  • Asian Cultural Extravaganzas: Graceful classical routines featuring authentic Thai, Korean, and Chinese courtly dance traditions and folklore.
World-Class Staging

Theatre & Production Features

  • State-of-the-Art Sound & Lighting: Cutting-edge surround sound acoustics, moving spotlight rigs, and dynamic projection backdrops.

  • Post-Show Courtyard Meet & Greet: Opportunity to take memorable souvenir photos with the glamourous stars in the outdoor theatre plaza.
