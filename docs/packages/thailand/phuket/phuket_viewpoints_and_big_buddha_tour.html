Tour Overview & Description

45-Meter Marbled Buddha atop Nakkerd Hill, Grand Pagoda of Wat Chalong & Coastal Lookout Panoramas

Immerse yourself in Phuket's most awe-inspiring scenic vistas and sacred spiritual landmarks on this guided half-day viewpoints and temple excursion. Designed for travelers seeking breathtaking panoramic photography and deep cultural discovery, this tour connects the island's most iconic coastal outlooks and monumental religious shrines.

Ascend the lush summit of Nakkerd Hill to stand beneath the majestic 45-meter Big Buddha statue, marveling at sweeping 360-degree views across Chalong Bay, Kata, and Rawai. Pay homage at Wat Chalong, the island's spiritual heart, before continuing to the wind-swept coastal plateaus of Windmill Viewpoint and Promthep Cape.

Places Covered & Highlights

Experience the highlights and sights included on this journey

Spiritual Landmarks

Sacred Island Monuments

  • The Great Buddha of Phuket: The towering 45-meter white Burmese marble statue atop Nakkerd Hill with panoramic 360-degree vistas across southern Phuket.
  • Wat Chalong (Wat Chaithararam): Phuket's largest Buddhist temple complex, featuring the Grand Pagoda enshrining a holy bone relic of Lord Buddha.
  • Buddhist Blessing & Merit Making: Experience traditional temple rituals, light lotus incense, and receive a ceremonial blessing from temple monks.
Dramatic Vistas

Iconic Coastal Lookouts

  • Promthep Cape (Laem Promthep): Thailand's most famous coastal headland jutting into the Andaman Sea with sweeping views of Koh Racha and Koh Hey.
  • Windmill Viewpoint: Hilltop lookout perched above Ya Nui Beach featuring rotating wind turbines and emerald-blue water views.
  • Karon Viewpoint (Kho Sam Haad): Legendary vantage point framing Kata Noi, Kata, and Karon crescent beaches in a single vista.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about this Phuket activity

Both the Big Buddha and Wat Chalong are active Buddhist religious sanctuaries. Visitors must dress respectfully: shoulders, chest, and knees must be covered. Sleeveless shirts, crop tops, short shorts, and sheer clothing are strictly prohibited. Free sarongs or wraps are provided at the entrance if required.

The main lower plateau and viewpoint terrace surrounding the Big Buddha are wheelchair accessible via paved pathways and ramps. The upper terrace immediately beneath the statue requires climbing stairs.

The morning tour offers clearer morning skies, less road traffic, and gentle lighting at Wat Chalong. The afternoon tour is immensely popular for capturing the golden sunset glow at Promthep Cape and Windmill Viewpoint.

Wild macaques occasionally inhabit the forested trees along Nakkerd Hill and Promthep Cape. We advise guests to admire them from a distance and avoid feeding them or carrying exposed food.
