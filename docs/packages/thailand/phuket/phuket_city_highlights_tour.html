Tour Overview & Description

Karon Viewpoint, Big Buddha, Wat Chalong, Cashew Nut Factory & Historic Old Town

Discover the vibrant culture, rich maritime history, and breathtaking coastal vistas of Thailand's largest island on the Phuket City Highlights & Landmarks Tour. Designed as the perfect introductory excursion for first-time visitors and families, this comprehensive half-day tour takes you beyond the beaches to explore the island's most revered cultural monuments and panoramic vantage points.

Travel in comfortable air-conditioned transport accompanied by a knowledgeable local guide. From the three-bay vista at Karon Viewpoint and the spiritual serenity of Wat Chalong to the pastel Sino-Portuguese shophouses of Phuket Old Town and artisanal cashew nut processing, you will experience the authentic heritage and natural beauty of Phuket in a relaxed, well-paced itinerary.

Places Covered & Highlights

Experience the highlights and sights included on this journey

Culture & Heritage

Sacred Temples & Historic Quarters

  • Wat Chalong (Wat Chaithararam): Phuket's most revered temple complex, housing the 60-meter-tall Grand Pagoda (Chedi) containing an authentic bone relic fragment of Lord Buddha.
  • Phuket Old Town Heritage Street: Stroll past beautifully preserved 19th-century Sino-Portuguese shophouses, heritage cafes, vibrant street murals, and historic shrines along Thalang Road.
  • Cashew Nut Processing Factory: Discover the fascinating cultivation and processing of local cashew nuts with tastings of various roasted and honey-glazed flavors.
Panoramic Lookouts

Coastal Panoramas & Landmarks

  • Karon Viewpoint (Kho Sam Haad): Famous hillside lookout offering a postcard-perfect view of three iconic beaches: Kata Noi, Kata Yai, and Karon Beach.
  • Big Buddha of Phuket (Nakkerd Hill): Marvel at the monumental 45-meter white Burmese marble Buddha statue commanding sweeping 360-degree views over Chalong Bay and Kata.
  • Windmill Viewpoint & Yanui Vista: Scenic overlook between Ya Nui and Nai Harn beaches featuring rotating white windmills and crystal-clear turquoise waters.
