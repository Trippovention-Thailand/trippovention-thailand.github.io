Tour Overview & Description

European-Standard Marine Mammal Performance, Acrobatic Dolphins & Fur Seals

Pattaya Dolphinarium is widely recognized as one of Southeast Asia's modern, European-standard marine mammal facilities. Designed by international marine specialists, the state-of-the-art amphitheatre hosts world-class acrobatic displays that showcase the intelligence, agility, and playful nature of dolphins and fur seals.

During the 50-minute performance, audience members are treated to synchronized leaps, aerial spins, hoop jumps, balancing acts, and musical interactions between marine mammals and their passionate trainers. Optional VIP seating puts you right at the edge of the water for splash-zone excitement.

Spectacular Show

Performance Highlights

  • Bottlenose Dolphin Acrobatics: High-flying leaps reaching several meters into the air, tail-walks, basketball hoops, and synchronized underwater choreography.
  • South American Fur Seals: Charming comedic routines, flipper clapping, ball balancing, and interactions with the audience by adorable trained fur seals.
  • Trainer Synergy: Witness the deep bond of trust and positive reinforcement training between international coaches and their marine partners.
Memorable Upgrades

Special Programs

  • Photo with Dolphins: Cherish a close-up keepsake photo sitting right beside a smiling dolphin on the stage platform after the show.
  • Swimming with Dolphins: Unique once-in-a-lifetime program allowing guests to swim and play in the pool alongside friendly dolphins (advance booking required).
  • Covered Amphitheatre: Modern sheltered stadium seating ensuring 100% weather protection from sun or rain with clear sightlines.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about this Pattaya activity

What is the difference between Regular, Deluxe, and VIP seats?▾

Regular seats occupy the upper rows (Rows 6–7). Deluxe seats are located in the middle rows (Rows 3–5) offering balanced panoramic views. VIP seats are in Rows 1–2 right at the pool's edge with prime viewing (complimentary rain ponchos provided).

Can children swim with the dolphins?▾

Yes, children aged 6 and above can participate in the 'Swimming with Dolphins' program accompanied by an adult, provided they are comfortable in deep water.

Are photos permitted during the performance?▾

Yes, non-commercial photography and video recording from your seat are permitted throughout the performance. Flash photography is prohibited for animal safety.

Is the venue sheltered from rain and sun?▾

Yes, the entire 1,000-seat amphitheatre is fully sheltered with a modern canopy roof, providing comfortable shaded seating in any weather.
