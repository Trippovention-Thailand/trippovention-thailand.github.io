Tour Overview & Description

Rugged Dirt Trails, Mud Tracks, Pineapple Plantations & Countryside Vistas

Leave the crowded beaches behind and explore the wild, rugged side of Pattaya on an action-packed guided ATV quad-bike safari through Chonburi's picturesque countryside. Navigating deep jungle tracks, sandy trails, rutted hillsides, and sprawling pineapple and coconut plantations, this tour is pure off-road adrenaline.

Begin with a safety briefing and riding tutorial on a dedicated practice track to master your automatic, user-friendly ATV. Accompanied by professional lead and sweep trail guides, you will traverse diverse terrains tailored to your skill level, complete with splashing water crossings, panoramic hill lookouts, and mud-churning excitement.

Trail Highlights

  • Jungle Single-Tracks: Twist and turn through dense tropical jungle foliage, bamboo tunnels, and shaded tree canopies.
  • Mud Pits & Water Splashes: Splash through natural mud ruts, muddy pools, and water crossings for maximum off-road thrill.
  • Hilltop Viewpoint: Ascend to elevated scenic ridgelines offering sweeping panoramic views across Pattaya's coastal hinterland.
Pro Equipment

Equipment & Safety

  • Automatic 300cc/450cc Quad Bikes: Modern, fully automatic, easy-to-ride quad bikes suitable for beginners and experienced riders alike.
  • Full Safety Gear: Certified safety helmets, goggles, and face dust masks provided for all participants.
  • Dedicated Practice Circuit: Test track session under guide supervision to build throttle, braking, and steering confidence before hitting the trail.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about this Pattaya activity

Do I need a motorcycle or driver's license to drive an ATV?▾

No formal driver's license is required. The quad bikes are fully automatic (simple thumb throttle and hand brakes). All riders receive instruction and practice time before entering trails.

Can children ride as passengers?▾

Yes, children aged 5 to 14 can ride comfortably as passengers on a tandem ATV behind an adult driver or professional guide at a discounted passenger rate.

What happens if it rains?▾

Rain makes ATV riding even more fun! Rainy conditions create thrilling mud tracks, deeper water splashes, and cooler temperatures. Tours operate rain or shine, except during severe tropical thunderstorms.

Are shower facilities available after the ride?▾

Yes, the off-road base camp provides clean shower cubicles, changing rooms, and lockers so you can wash off mud and change into fresh dry clothes before your hotel transfer.
