Tour Overview & Description

Discover Pattaya's cultural landmarks, sweeping coastal panoramas, and evening cabaret

Explore the vibrant coastal city of Pattaya on a comprehensive private sightseeing tour that showcases its most revered cultural landmarks, scenic hillside viewpoints, and world-class theatrical entertainment. Enjoy the personalized comfort and flexibility of an air-conditioned private vehicle and dedicated driver.

Ascend to the sacred Big Buddha Temple (Wat Phra Yai) atop Pratumnak Hill to admire the monumental golden Buddha statue and intricate dragon staircases. Soak in 360-degree crescent bay panoramas from Khao Phra Tamnak Viewpoint, cruise down the lively Pattaya Beach Road, and explore Thailand's artisanal craftsmanship at Gems Gallery Pattaya. In the evening, witness the breathtaking Colosseum Cabaret Show—staged inside a colossal Roman-inspired amphitheater featuring 14 kaleidoscopic acts, opulent costumes, and international choreography.

Places Covered

Pattaya's iconic landmarks and evening entertainment stops

Spiritual & Scenic Sights

City Landmarks & Vistas

  • Big Buddha Temple (Wat Phra Yai): Golden 18-meter-tall Buddha seated atop lush Pratumnak Hill.
  • Khao Phra Tamnak Viewpoint: The most celebrated scenic lookout overlooking the crescent curve of Pattaya Bay.
  • Pattaya Beach Road: Scenic coastal drive through the bustling heart of Pattaya's seaside promenade.
  • Gems Gallery Pattaya: The world's largest jewelry showroom with an immersive dark-ride geological exhibition.
Evening Entertainment

Colosseum Cabaret Show

  • Roman-Inspired Amphitheater: Grand neoclassical architecture with 1,000 tiered seats and hydraulic stage.
  • 14 Diverse Musical Acts: Glamorous theatrical vignettes from Broadway, China, Europe, and Siam.
  • Sensational Costumes: Hand-stitched crystal gowns, feathered crowns, and dramatic couture.
  • Comfortable Private Transit: Relaxed transfers connecting all sightseeing stops and show venue.
