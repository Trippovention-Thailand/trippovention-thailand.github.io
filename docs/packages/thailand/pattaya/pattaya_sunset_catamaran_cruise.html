Luxury Sailing Catamaran, Koh Phai Snorkeling, Sunset BBQ & Golden Hour

⏱️

Meet-up Location

Tour Overview & Description

Luxury Sailing Catamaran, Koh Phai Snorkeling, Sunset BBQ & Golden Hour

Experience the glamour and romance of the Gulf of Thailand aboard an elegant luxury sailing catamaran departing from Ocean Marina Yacht Club, Southeast Asia's premier marina facility. Leave the noisy city behind as billowed sails catch the afternoon sea breeze.

Lounge on spacious bow trampolines, swim and snorkel around uninhabited tropical islands with crystal-clear waters, try paddleboarding or onboard fishing, and savor a freshly prepared barbecue seafood dinner as the sun sinks into the horizon, bathing the sea in vivid amber, magenta, and gold.

Places Covered & Highlights

Experience the highlights and sights included on this journey

Tropical Archipelago

Island & Marine Exploration

  • Koh Phai (Bamboo Island): Protected uninhabited island under the Royal Thai Navy featuring pristine coral reefs, sea anemones, and white sands.
  • Snorkeling & Paddleboarding: Complimentary snorkel masks, fins, stand-up paddleboards, and inflatable floating mats.
  • Hand-Line Fishing: Try traditional hand-line fishing from the stern under the guidance of friendly crew members.
Golden Hour

Onboard Luxury & Sunset

  • Bow Trampoline Sundecks: Relax on open-air netting suspended directly above the ocean spray as the catamaran glides over waves.
  • Fresh Seafood BBQ Buffet: Delicious onboard buffet featuring grilled prawns, chicken satay, fried rice, fresh salads, and seasonal fruits.
  • Sunset Golden Hour: Unforgettable panoramic views of the glowing sun sinking into the Gulf of Thailand with chillout background music.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about this Pattaya activity

Where does the catamaran depart from?▾

The cruise boards at Ocean Marina Yacht Club in Na Jomtien, Pattaya. Our team can arrange round-trip hotel transfers directly to the marina pier.

Is the cruise suitable for non-swimmers?▾

Yes! Sailing catamarans are twin-hulled vessels known for extreme stability and very little rocking motion. Non-swimmers can relax comfortably on sunbeds, enjoy the music, and use life jackets on floating mats.

Can I purchase alcoholic drinks on board?▾

Yes, the yacht features a licensed bar stocked with cold beers, wine, sparkling champagne, and handcrafted cocktails at reasonable prices.

What happens in case of bad weather?▾

Catamaran sails are subject to marine safety conditions. If seas are deemed unsafe by the marine department, the trip is rescheduled or refunded in full.
