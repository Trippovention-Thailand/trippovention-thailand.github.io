Tour Overview & Description

Thailand's Largest Water Park, 21 World-Class Slides & Natural Crystal-Clear Springs

Ranked consistently as the #1 water park in Thailand and among the top 10 water parks in the world by TripAdvisor, Ramayana Water Park spans over 45 acres of breathtaking natural greenery nestled between Silverlake Vineyard and Khao Chi Chan Buddha Mountain.

Ramayana features 21 premium WhiteWater West slides, crystal-clear water supplied from the park's own natural freshwater wells with state-of-the-art filtration, two expansive children's aqua playgrounds, a 600-meter lazy river passing ancient ruins, and a dual-wave beach pool. Whether seeking adrenaline-pumping drop slides or relaxing in a private VIP cabana, Ramayana guarantees a premier family day out.

Places Covered & Highlights

Experience the highlights and sights included on this journey

World-Class Thrills

Adrenaline Slides

  • Aqualoop & Freefall: Step into the launch capsule and feel the floor drop out beneath you into a near-vertical 360-degree looping chute.
  • Python & Aquaconda: Massive interconnected tunnel tubes featuring huge oscillating wall climbs and exhilarating sudden drops on multi-person rafts.
  • Boomerango & Spiral: Propel up a vertical curved wall for a moment of zero-gravity weightlessness before splashing into the lower pool.
Family Leisure

Relaxation & Kids Play

  • 600-Meter Lazy River: Drift gently through lush tropical gardens, waterfall grottos, and mystical ruins of an ancient lost city.
  • Double Wave Pool: Massive split beach pool generating gentle swells on one side and dramatic rolling waves on the other.
  • Aquasplash & River Slide: Two dedicated interactive water splash zones designed specifically for toddlers and young children with zero-depth entry.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about this Pattaya activity

Why is Ramayana considered the cleanest water park in Thailand?▾

Ramayana sources all its water from on-site deep mineral wells and purifies it through advanced multi-stage filtration systems without relying on harsh chemicals, making the water gentle on sensitive skin and eyes.

Are there height or weight restrictions on the big slides?▾

Yes, most high-thrill slides require a minimum height of 122 cm. Dedicated children's zones accommodate young adventurers under 122 cm with gentler slides and splash fountains.

Can we rent private cabanas at the park?▾

Yes, private shaded poolside cabanas equipped with ceiling fans, electrical outlets, and towel service can be booked for families desiring extra privacy and comfort.

Are vegetarian, vegan, and halal meals available?▾

Yes, Ramayana's food court features certified halal food stations, vegetarian, Western, and authentic Thai cuisine prepared fresh daily.
