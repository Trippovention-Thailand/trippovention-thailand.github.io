Tour Overview & Description

Interactive 3D Optical Illusion Gallery, AR Smartphone App & Themed Zones

Art in Paradise Pattaya is Thailand's first and largest interactive 3D art museum, spanning 5,800 square meters across an air-conditioned modern facility in North Pattaya. Created by talented Korean and Thai artists, the gallery features more than 140 photorealistic trompe-l'œil paintings.

Unlike traditional museums where touching art is forbidden, visitors here become part of the masterpieces! Stand on marked camera photo spots to capture mind-bending optical illusions. Download the museum's companion AR (Augmented Reality) app on your smartphone to watch paintings come to life with animations and sound effects on your video screen.

Creative Photo Fun

Interactive Themed Zones

  • Underwater World Zone: Pose inside the jaws of a prehistoric megalodon shark or swim alongside giant sea turtles and mermaids.
  • Wildlife & Safari Zone: Escape charging rhinoceroses, touch the trunk of an African elephant, or cross a rickety rope bridge over a canyon.
  • Classic Masterpiece Parody: Insert yourself humorously into famous Western paintings including the Mona Lisa, The Creation of Adam, and Van Gogh's bedroom.
Illusion Art

Surreal & Fantasy Worlds

  • Fantasy & Magic Realm: Float inside giant crystal bubbles, fly on flying carpets, or shrink into a miniature dollhouse world.
  • Dinosaur & Prehistoric Zone: Flee from towering T-Rex jaws crashing through concrete museum walls.
  • AR Video Interaction: Use the dedicated AR app to record videos where dragons breathe real fire and waterfalls roar realistically around you.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about this Pattaya activity

How do the 3D photos work?▾

Each mural has specific camera photo markers taped on the floor showing the photographer exactly where to stand and what camera angle to hold to produce the realistic 3D perspective effect.

Is the museum air-conditioned?▾

Yes, the entire 5,800-square-meter complex is fully air-conditioned, making it a fantastic rainy-day activity or cool afternoon escape from the tropical heat.

How long does a visit usually take?▾

Most visitors spend between 1.5 to 2.5 hours posing, taking photos, and recording interactive AR videos across the six gallery zones.

Is Art in Paradise suitable for all ages?▾

Yes, toddlers, teenagers, couples, and grandparents all thoroughly enjoy the creative, hilarious photo setups.
