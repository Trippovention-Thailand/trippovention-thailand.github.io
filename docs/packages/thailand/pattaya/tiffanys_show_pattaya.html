Tour Overview & Description

The Original Transgender Cabaret of Southeast Asia, Opulent Costumes & Broadway Stages

Established in 1974, Tiffany's Show Pattaya is the legendary pioneer of transgender cabaret entertainment in Southeast Asia. Voted among the top 10 world cabaret shows, Tiffany's has dazzled international audiences for over five decades with theatrical artistry, grandeur, and precision choreography.

Staged inside a palatial 1,000-seat theatre equipped with advanced acoustics, hydraulic stages, and LED backdrops, over 100 glamorous performers present an international songbook spanning Broadway classics, Chinese imperial legends, Bollywood extravaganzas, and traditional Thai court dance.

World-Class Repertoire

Musical & Theatrical Acts

  • Imperial China & Empress Act: Majestic palace sets with intricate silk costumes, golden dragons, and classical fan dancing.

Grand Production

Theatre & Production

  • Palatial 1,000-Seat Theatre: Opulent European-style auditorium with plush tiered seating, state-of-the-art surround sound, and laser lighting.
  • Elaborate Feather Costumes: Spectacular handmade costumes adorned with hundreds of thousands of Swarovski crystals and ostrich feathers.
  • Post-Show Photo Courtyard: Meet the star performers in the open-air fountain plaza after the finale for unforgettable souvenir photos.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about this Pattaya activity

What are the differences between seating tiers at Tiffany's?▾

Mezzanine seats are on the elevated balcony. VIP seats are located on the ground floor providing excellent direct sightlines. VIP Gold seats occupy the front-central rows closest to the stage with the best view of detailed costumes and makeup.

Is photography allowed during the show?▾

Photography and video recording inside the auditorium are strictly prohibited during the performance to respect copyright and artist safety. However, photo sessions in the outdoor courtyard after the show are welcomed!

Is Tiffany's Show suitable for families with children?▾

Yes, Tiffany's is a glamorous, family-friendly celebration of music, cultural dance, and theatrical costuming with no vulgar or adult content.

How early should we arrive before showtime?▾

We recommend arriving at the theatre lobby at least 20 to 30 minutes before the scheduled curtain call to redeem your tickets and enjoy your welcome beverage.
