Tour Overview & Description

World's First Sony Columbia Pictures Theme & Water Park in Na Jomtien, Pattaya

Immerse yourself in world-class cinematic thrills at Columbia Pictures Aquaverse, the planet's very first fully branded Sony Columbia Pictures theme and water park. Located in Na Jomtien just minutes south of Pattaya, this massive entertainment destination brings iconic blockbuster movies to life across eight immersive themed zones.

From high-speed water slides themed around Ghostbusters, Jumanji, and Zombieland to the Southeast Asia's largest wave pool with a giant outdoor cinema screen, Aquaverse delivers non-stop excitement for thrill-seekers, kids, and movie lovers alike. Enjoy themed food zones, miniature golf, Bad Boys Miami go-karts, and live character meet-and-greets throughout the day.

Places Covered & Highlights

Experience the highlights and sights included on this journey

High-Thrill Rides

Top Blockbuster Zones

  • Ghostbusters Proton Stream: World's first water coaster in a dark enclosed sphere featuring twists, turns, and ghost-hunting visual projections.
  • Jumanji Jungle Adventure: Viper-themed wild water slides shooting through jungle canopies into a massive splashdown basin.
  • Zombieland Daredevil Drop: Exhilarating 18-meter vertical trapdoor drop slide reaching breakneck speeds into a high-g deceleration runout.
Family Entertainment

Family & Entertainment Zones

  • Hotel Transylvania Water Castle: Southeast Asia's largest kid-friendly splash structure featuring 9 water slides, 100+ water play elements, and giant dump buckets.
  • Mega Wave Pool & Aquaverse Cinema: Catch movie screenings, DJ music sets, and live concerts while floating on rubber tubes in the massive wave pool.
  • Bad Boys Raceway: High-speed outdoor electric go-kart track designed with neon-lit Miami street racing vibes.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about this Pattaya activity

What is the difference between adult and child tickets?▾

Adult tickets apply to guests aged 13 and above. Child tickets apply to children aged 3 to 12. Infants aged 0 to 2 enter completely free of charge when accompanied by a paying adult.

Can visitors bring outside food or drinks into the park?▾

Outside food and beverages are not permitted inside Aquaverse, with the exception of baby formula and medical dietary necessities. The park features multiple movie-themed dining options offering Thai, Western, and halal choices.

Are towels and storage lockers available for rent?▾

Yes, electronic RFID lockers and clean rental towels are available near the changing rooms for a nominal daily fee.

Is Columbia Pictures Aquaverse suitable for non-swimmers?▾

Yes! Certified life vests are provided free of charge, and professional lifeguards supervise all pools and slide splash basins continuously. Zones like Hotel Transylvania and the Lazy River are ideal for guests of all swimming abilities.
