Tour Overview & Description

Ethical Rescued Elephant Interaction, Fruit Preparation, Mud Spa & River Walk

Immerse yourself in a compassionate, ethical wildlife encounter at Pattaya Elephant Sanctuary. Dedicated to the rescue, rehabilitation, and respectful care of retired elephants from the logging and tourism industries, the sanctuary operates on a strictly hands-off, no-riding philosophy.

Spend your morning or afternoon learning about Asian elephant biology and behavior from conservation guides. Prepare fresh dietary supplements of bananas, sugarcane, and nutrient herbal balls, feed the gentle giants by hand, and walk alongside them to a natural mud spa and freshwater bathing pool.

Places Covered & Highlights

Experience the highlights and sights included on this journey

Hands-On Care

Sanctuary Activities

  • Elephant Kitchen: Chop fresh bananas, sugarcane, and blend herbal rice balls tailored for elder rescued elephants with worn teeth.
  • Gentle Feeding Session: Experience the wonder of interacting with elephants up close as they take treats directly from your hands with their sensitive trunks.
  • Mud Spa & Skin Care: Apply cooling natural mineral mud to elephant skin to protect them from insects and the tropical sun.
Ethical Freedom

Natural Living

  • Freshwater Bathing Pool: Join the elephants in the natural pond or watch them happily splash, roll, and submerge themselves in the water.
  • Jungle Canopy Walk: Accompany the herd as they wander freely through open bamboo groves and forage naturally.
  • Educational Workshop: Insightful presentation on elephant rescue, conservation challenges, and the evolution of animal welfare in Thailand.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about this Pattaya activity

Does the sanctuary permit elephant riding?▾

No, under no circumstances. We strictly partner with authentic ethical sanctuaries that prioritize elephant health, autonomy, and dignity. Activities focus solely on observing, feeding, and caring for rescued animals.

Are there shower facilities to clean off mud after the bath?▾

Yes! The sanctuary provides clean private showers, soap, shampoo, and fresh towels so you can comfortably rinse off before enjoying your meal and returning to your hotel.

Is the program safe for children?▾

Yes, the rescued elephants at the sanctuary are gentle, habituated to human presence, and supervised by experienced mahouts at all times. Children of all ages love feeding and touching the elephants.

What should I wear for this experience?▾

Wear clothes you don't mind getting dirty. The sanctuary provides lightweight protective cotton shirts to wear over your swimwear during the mud spa session.
