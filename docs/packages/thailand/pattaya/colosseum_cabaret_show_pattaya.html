Tour Overview & Description

Roman Amphitheatre-Inspired Theatre, 14 Themed Acts & High-Tech Stage Effects

Housed in a breathtaking 17-acre complex inspired by the ancient Roman Colosseum, Colosseum Cabaret Pattaya is the largest and most architecturally striking theatre in Pattaya. With 1,250 amphitheatre seats arranged in a progressive horseshoe curve, every seat guarantees unobstructed sightlines.

Colosseum presents 14 dynamic, kaleidoscope acts blending contemporary pop, traditional Asian cultural narratives, European opera, and laugh-out-loud comedy. Powered by dynamic hydraulic lift stages, computerized lighting, and opulent custom wardrobe, it is a magnificent evening showpiece.

Places Covered & Highlights

Experience the highlights and sights included on this journey

14 Spectacular Scenes

Kaleidoscopic Acts

  • Love on Top & Disco Finale: High-energy pop tributes with breathtaking dance routines and shimmering sequined ensembles.
  • The Legend of Silk & Lotus: Enchanting traditional Asian choreography celebrating Chinese court beauty and lotus blossom mythology.
  • Roman Gladiator Overture: Dramatic theatrical opening act paying homage to ancient Rome and the iconic Colosseum architecture.
Grand Amphitheatre

Theatre Architecture

  • Horseshoe Amphitheatre Design: Tiered circular seating layout ensuring superior sightlines and acoustic resonance from every angle.
  • Hydraulic Stage Machinery: Multi-level mechanical stages that raise and lower elaborate sets and cast members seamlessly.
  • Courtyard Plaza Meet & Greet: Spacious Roman-columned forecourt where guests can meet performers for photographs after the show.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about this Pattaya activity

How does Colosseum compare to Alcazar or Tiffany's?▾

Colosseum features Pattaya's largest and newest purpose-built amphitheatre, with an iconic Roman exterior and slightly steeper tiered seating, meaning even rear seats enjoy completely unobstructed views over heads in front.

Can we combine the Colosseum Show with dinner?▾

Yes! Located right on Thepprasit Road, many visitors pair the 06:00 PM show with an evening stroll and dinner at the famous Thepprasit Weekend Night Market nearby.

Is Colosseum wheelchair accessible?▾

Yes, the theatre features wheelchair ramps at the entrance and dedicated accessible seating areas on the ground floor.

Are children permitted to attend?▾

Yes, Colosseum is an all-ages theatrical musical show suitable for children, couples, and seniors alike.
