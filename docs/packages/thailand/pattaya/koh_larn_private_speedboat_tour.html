Tour Overview & Description

Exclusive Island Charter, Hidden Beaches, Crystal Waters & Custom Itinerary

Escape the crowded public ferries and rigid tour schedules with an exclusive private speedboat charter from Pattaya to Coral Island (Koh Larn). Cruising across turquoise waters at exhilarating speeds, your private vessel reaches pristine white-sand shores in just 15 to 20 minutes.

Enjoy complete freedom to customize your island day. Spend your morning swimming and relaxing at tranquil Tien Beach or secluded Nual Beach (Monkey Beach), cruise around hidden coves for reef snorkeling, dine at scenic seaside seafood restaurants, and add high-octane water sports on your own terms.

Places Covered & Highlights

Experience the highlights and sights included on this journey

Tropical Beaches

Island Highlights

  • Tien Beach: Scenic curved beach famous for crystal-clear aquamarine water, swaying palm trees, and charming wooden boardwalks.
  • Samae Beach: Expansive soft white sand bay offering relaxed sunbeds, beachfront restaurants, and solar-powered eco windmill views.
  • Nual Beach (Monkey Beach): Tranquil secluded bay at the southern tip of Koh Larn where wild monkeys frequently visit the rocky shoreline.
VIP Freedom

Private Charter Perks

  • Exclusive Twin-Engine Speedboat: Private vessel chartered solely for your party with dedicated captain and crew.
  • Snorkeling at Coral Coves: Anchor at quiet reefs around Koh Larn or Koh Sak to snorkel among damselfish and parrotfish.
  • Flexible Timetable: Choose your preferred departure and return times without waiting for group tour buses.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about this Pattaya activity

How many passengers can fit on the private speedboat?▾

Our standard charter package accommodates up to 6 passengers comfortably. For larger groups or extended families, we offer 15-passenger and 30-passenger speedboats on request.

Can we choose which beaches to visit on Koh Larn?▾

Yes! That is the primary advantage of a private charter. You can spend more time at quiet beaches like Tien or Nual Beach, or head to Tawaen Beach for water sports.

Can we bring our own food and alcoholic beverages on board?▾

Yes, you are welcome to bring snacks, beer, wine, or soft drinks on board without corkage fees. We provide an ice-filled cooler box.

What if someone gets seasick?▾

The crossing to Koh Larn takes only 15 to 20 minutes across generally sheltered Gulf waters. Motion sickness pills are available upon boarding if requested.
