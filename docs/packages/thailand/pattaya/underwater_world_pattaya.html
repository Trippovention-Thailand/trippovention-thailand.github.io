Tour Overview & Description

100-Meter Acrylic Tunnel, 5,000+ Marine Animals, Touch Pools & Shark Feeding

Underwater World Pattaya is Thailand's first modern indoor oceanarium, home to an extraordinary collection of over 5,000 aquatic animals representing 500 diverse marine species native to the Gulf of Thailand and the South China Sea.

The centerpiece of the aquarium is a 100-meter-long, 180-degree curved acrylic pedestrian tunnel divided into three distinct underwater zones: Coral Reef Zone, Shark and Stingray Zone, and Giant of Siam Zone featuring a submerged ancient shipwreck. Watch blacktip reef sharks, nurse sharks, and majestic eagle rays glide effortlessly overhead.

Places Covered & Highlights

Experience the highlights and sights included on this journey

Marine Life

Tunnel Zones

  • Shark & Ray Zone: Experience heart-pounding views of apex predators, nurse sharks, shovel-nose rays, and blacktip reef sharks swimming overhead.
  • Coral Reef Zone: Vibrant living display of tropical corals, clownfish, angelfish, and delicate sea anemones in brilliant colors.
  • Giant of Siam Zone: Themed around a mystical sunken shipwreck, home to giant freshwater arapaimas, Mekong giant catfish, and sea turtles.
Hands-On Fun

Interactive Encounters

  • Touch Pool Experience: Safely touch sea cucumbers, blue-spotted stingrays, starfish, and horseshoe crabs under marine staff guidance.
  • Scheduled Feeding Shows: Watch scuba divers swim inside the main oceanarium tanks to hand-feed hungry sharks and stingrays twice daily.
  • Koi Fish Bottle Feeding: Delight young children with the unique experience of nursing hungry koi fish with specially prepared milk bottles.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about this Pattaya activity

When are the shark feeding shows held?▾

Diver feeding presentations typically take place daily at 11:00 AM, 02:00 PM, and 04:00 PM in the Shark & Stingray Zone and Coral Reef Zone.

How long does it take to see the whole aquarium?▾

Most visitors spend between 1.5 to 2 hours exploring the walk-through tunnels, touch pool, and watching the scheduled feeding demonstrations.

Is Underwater World fully wheelchair and stroller accessible?▾

Yes, the facility is designed on a gentle continuous incline with zero stairs, making it 100% accessible for strollers, wheelchairs, and senior travelers.

Can certified divers swim with the sharks here?▾

Yes, Underwater World offers an exclusive 'Dive with Sharks & Rays' program for certified scuba divers. Advance booking through Trippovention is required.
