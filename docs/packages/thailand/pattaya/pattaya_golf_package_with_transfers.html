Tour Overview & Description

18-Hole Championship Round, Siam Country Club / Chee Chan, Caddy & Buggy Included

Pattaya is globally renowned as the 'Golf Capital of the East', boasting over 20 world-class championship golf courses designed by legends including Jack Nicklaus, Pete Dye, and Gary Player. Our comprehensive golf packages provide a seamless, VIP sporting experience for amateur and scratch golfers alike.

Enjoy guaranteed morning tee times at acclaimed courses such as Siam Country Club (host of the LPGA Thailand), Chee Chan Golf Resort (overlooking Buddha Mountain), or Phoenix Gold Golf & Country Club. Packages include private door-to-door luxury transport, your 18-hole green fee, motorized golf cart, and professional caddy service.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about this Pattaya activity

Can I rent golf clubs and shoes at the golf club?▾

Yes, all our partner championship clubs maintain pro shops offering top-tier rental sets (Callaway, Titleist, TaylorMade) for right- and left-handed players, as well as golf shoes in all sizes.

What is the customary tip for caddies in Thailand?▾

Caddies in Thailand provide exceptional service, from reading greens to marking balls and holding umbrellas. The customary cash tip is a minimum of 400 to 500 THB per 18-hole round, paid directly to your caddy at the 18th green.

What is the dress code on Pattaya golf courses?▾

Standard golf etiquette applies: collared golf shirts, tailored trousers or golf shorts, soft-spike golf shoes, and socks. Jeans, t-shirts, tank tops, and sandals are strictly prohibited on the course.

Can non-golfing spouses or companions ride along?▾

Yes, non-golfing companions can accompany players on the course as riders for a modest rider fee (covering insurance and buggy access).
