Tour Overview & Description

Escape the mainland hustle and relax on Pattaya's premier tropical island

Spend a memorable day at Coral Island (Koh Larn), Pattaya's most popular tropical offshore destination, renowned for its crystal-clear turquoise waters, powdery white sandy beaches, and vibrant shallow coral reefs. Located just 7.5 kilometers off the coast of Pattaya, Koh Larn offers a refreshing sanctuary of sunshine and sea.

Travel by high-speed speedboat across the Gulf of Thailand to the island's picturesque beaches. Enjoy ample leisure time swimming in warm gentle waves, sunbathing on provided beach loungers, or soaking up spectacular coastal vistas. Adventure lovers can add thrilling watersports such as parasailing over the bay, jet skiing, banana boat rides, or sea walking underwater. Conclude your island getaway with a delicious freshly prepared lunch before cruising back to Pattaya.

Island Highlights & Activities

Beach adventures and recreation awaiting you on Koh Larn

Tropical Beaches

Koh Larn Coastal Sights

  • Tawaen & Tien Beaches: Famous stretches of powdery white sand with clear swimming lagoons.
  • Speedboat Cruise: Scenic 20-minute speedboat crossing with panoramic vistas of Pattaya's coastline.
  • Coral Reefs: Shallow fringing coral reefs harboring tropical damselfish and parrotfish.
  • Beach Lounging: Relax on shaded deckchairs listening to the rhythmic ocean waves.
Island Thrills

Optional Water Sports (On-Site)

  • Parasailing: Soar high above Pattaya Bay tethered to a high-speed boat for bird's-eye views.
  • Jet Skiing: Carve across the waves on top-grade high-powered watercraft.
  • Banana Boat Rides: High-spirited inflatable ride perfect for families and friend groups.
  • Underwater Sea Walking: Walk along the seabed wearing a specialized helmet feeding schools of fish.
