Tour Overview & Description

Experience Pattaya's most exciting dual wildlife and marine adventure

Experience an unforgettable wildlife adventure in Pattaya combining the marine brilliance of the Pattaya Dolphinarium and the majestic big cats of Tiger Park Pattaya. Travel smoothly with private door-to-door hotel transfers in a modern air-conditioned vehicle.

Begin by watching the spectacular European-standard Dolphin Show, where playful bottlenose dolphins and charming South American fur seals execute breathtaking mid-air flips, synchronized swimming, hoop jumping, and interactive games that delight guests of all ages. Next, journey to Tiger Park Pattaya for a once-in-a-lifetime encounter with a healthy, well-cared-for Medium Tiger inside an enclosed natural habitat, guided safely by trained animal specialists. This tour offers the ultimate combination of high-energy marine entertainment and memorable animal photography.

Parks & Attractions Covered

Key experiences included on this wildlife excursion

Marine Wonders

Pattaya Dolphinarium

  • Acrobatic Stunt Routines: Highly trained dolphins performing 5-meter aerial jumps, flips, and spins.
  • Fur Seal Comedy: Playful South American sea lions demonstrating balancing tricks and funny antics.
  • Indoor Arena: High-standard amphitheater with crystal-clear view pools and stadium seating.
  • Musical Performances: Synchronized musical choreography and trainer-dolphin water skiing displays.
Big Cat Encounters

Tiger Park Pattaya

  • Medium Tiger Interaction: Enter an enclosure with an adolescent tiger under supervision of seasoned handlers.
  • Close-Up Photo Session: Stand or sit beside the majestic tiger for extraordinary commemorative photographs.
  • Safari Atmosphere: Observe tigers swimming, playing, and resting in naturalistic open enclosures.
  • Conservation Learning: Insights into feline nutrition, wellness care, and habitat enrichment.
