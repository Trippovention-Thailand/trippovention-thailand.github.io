Tour Overview & Description

Feel the adrenaline rush navigating Koh Samui's lush tropical wilderness

Experience the thrill of off-road adventure as you ride an ATV quad bike through Koh Samui's rugged jungle trails, scenic hills, and tropical landscapes. Guided by experienced professionals, this exciting one-hour adventure takes you across dirt tracks, lush plantations, and natural viewpoints, offering breathtaking panoramic views of the island.

Whether you're a beginner or an experienced rider, this ATV tour provides an exhilarating way to explore Koh Samui's untouched countryside while enjoying a safe, professionally monitored outdoor experience with complete safety equipment provided.

Places Covered

Off-road trails and scenic highlights explored during your quad bike ride

Wild Terrain

Jungle Trails & Tracks

  • Jungle Trails: Winding off-road tracks carved through Koh Samui's lush inland rainforest and bamboo groves.
  • Off-Road Adventure Tracks: Exciting terrain featuring dirt paths, river crossings, natural undulations, and mud splashes.
  • Guided Routes: Carefully mapped trails suited for all experience levels with professional trail leaders.
Scenic Panoramas

Plantations & Viewpoints

  • Tropical Plantations: Ride past flourishing coconut groves, tropical durian and rambutan plantations, and rubber trees.
  • Mountain Viewpoints: Elevated scenic stops overlooking Koh Samui's turquoise coastline and forested ridges.
  • Photo Stops: Capture stunning memories on your quad bike against scenic mountain panoramas.
