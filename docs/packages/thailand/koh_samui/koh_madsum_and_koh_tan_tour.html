Tour Overview & Description

A delightful half-day island getaway to Samui's southern neighboring paradises

Escape to two of Koh Samui's hidden island gems on this half-day speedboat adventure to Koh Madsum (Pig Island) and Koh Tan (Coral Island). Relax on the white sandy beaches of Koh Madsum, famous for its friendly free-roaming pigs and crystal-clear waters, before heading to Koh Tan, a tranquil island renowned for its vibrant coral reefs and excellent snorkeling opportunities.

Enjoy kayaking through calm turquoise waters, swim in the clear blue sea, and take in the breathtaking tropical scenery. This tour is perfect for families, couples, and nature lovers looking for a fun, photogenic, and relaxing island escape just a short boat ride from southern Samui.

Places Covered

Two contrasting tropical islands explored during your morning speedboat tour

Pig Island Highlight

Koh Madsum (Pig Island)

  • Beach Pigs: Meet, interact with, and photograph the famous friendly family of pigs roaming freely along the sandy shoreline.
  • Powder-Soft Beach: Relax under swaying coconut trees on peaceful white sand.
  • Calm Swimming Bay: Safe, shallow turquoise waters ideal for swimming and wading.
Coral Island Paradise

Koh Tan (Coral Island)

  • Coral Reef Snorkeling: Explore pristine coral gardens teeming with angelfish, parrotfish, and sea anemones.
  • Sea Kayaking: Paddle tranquil mangrove estuaries and secluded coves with included kayak gear.
  • Unspoiled Nature: Koh Tan is known for its peaceful absence of cars, preserving an untouched natural island ecosystem.
