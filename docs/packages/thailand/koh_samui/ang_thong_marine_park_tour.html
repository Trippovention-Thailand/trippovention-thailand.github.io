Tour Overview & Description

An extraordinary day trip to Thailand's most famous marine archipelago

Embark on an unforgettable full-day adventure to Ang Thong National Marine Park, a breathtaking archipelago of 42 limestone islands surrounded by the emerald waters of the Gulf of Thailand. Travel by speedboat to explore pristine beaches, hidden lagoons, and dramatic cliffs while enjoying snorkeling in crystal-clear waters teeming with marine life.

Paddle through tranquil bays by kayak, climb to the iconic viewpoint overlooking the Emerald Lake (Talay Nai), and relax on untouched sands. A delicious Thai buffet lunch is served during the tour, making this excursion perfect for nature lovers, adventure seekers, couples, and families looking to experience one of Thailand's most celebrated protected marine wonderlands.

Places Covered

Iconic marine sights and islands visited on this speedboat excursion

Archipelago Wonders

Islands & Natural Landmarks

  • Ang Thong National Marine Park: 42 uninhabited islands rising dramatically out of the azure Gulf of Thailand.
  • Koh Wua Talap: The park headquarters island featuring sweeping summit views and peaceful white sandy shores.
  • Emerald Lake (Talay Nai): A breathtaking enclosed saltwater lagoon surrounded by sheer limestone cliffs on Mae Koh Island.
  • Mae Koh Island: Dramatic cliffs and scenic staircases leading up to the legendary Emerald Lake viewpoint.
Marine Activities

Adventure & Recreation

  • Snorkeling Site: Vibrant coral reefs teeming with colorful tropical fish and marine life.
  • Kayaking Area: Paddle guided sea kayaks beneath overhangs, sea caves, and along dramatic karst shorelines.
  • Pristine Beaches: Ample leisure time for swimming, beachcombing, and sunbathing on soft powdery sands.
  • Scenic Speedboat Cruise: High-speed transfers through the archipelago with spectacular coastal vistas.
