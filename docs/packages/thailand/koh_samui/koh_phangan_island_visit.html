Tour Overview & Description

Experience Koh Phangan's serene beaches and tropical charm at your own pace

Discover the laid-back charm and natural beauty of Koh Phangan on a full-day island visit from Koh Samui. Travel comfortably by ferry while enjoying scenic views of the Gulf of Thailand before exploring the island's pristine beaches, lush tropical landscapes, local markets, and cultural attractions at your own pace.

Whether you wish to relax by the turquoise sea, visit charming beachfront cafés, explore hidden bays, or experience the island's peaceful, bohemian atmosphere beyond its famous Full Moon Party, this excursion offers the perfect seamless day trip with private round-trip hotel transfers included.

Places Covered

Key destinations and free-time exploration areas during your day trip

Scenic Transit & Port

Gulf Crossing & Thong Sala

  • Scenic Ferry Cruise: Relax on an open-deck or air-conditioned ferry voyage across the picturesque waters separating Samui and Phangan.
  • Thong Sala Pier: The lively main gateway to Koh Phangan featuring welcoming harborside cafés, fruit markets, and local shops.
  • Private Transfers: Private door-to-door vehicle transfers between your Samui hotel and the ferry pier on both journeys.
Self-Paced Island Discovery

Beaches & Local Areas

  • Selected Beaches: Choose your favorite spot—from peaceful Haad Yao and Salad Beach to vibrant Baan Tai or world-famous Haad Rin.
  • Coastal Cafés & Beach Clubs: Savor fresh tropical coconuts, seafood, and Thai culinary specialties right on the sand.
  • Flexible Free Time: Spend 5 to 7 full hours exploring the island independently according to your personal interests.
