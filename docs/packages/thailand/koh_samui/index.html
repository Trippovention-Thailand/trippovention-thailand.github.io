Frequently Asked Questions About Koh Samui Tours

Quick facts on island sightseeing, boat tours, day trips, and transfers

What are the top sightseeing tours in Koh Samui?

Top experiences include the full-day Ang Thong National Marine Park speedboat tour (42 limestone islands & Emerald Lake), half-day Koh Madsum Pig Island & Koh Tan snorkeling, the Big Buddha & Wat Plai Laem City Tour, and off-road ATV jungle riding.

How early should I book Koh Samui tours?

We recommend booking 24 to 48 hours ahead, particularly during peak dry season (December–April) to secure speedboat seats and preferred departure times.

Are hotel pick-up and drop-off included?

Yes! All our Koh Samui sightseeing tours and island boat trips include round-trip hotel transfers from Chaweng, Lamai, Bophut, Choeng Mon, and surrounding areas.

Can I take a day trip from Koh Samui to Koh Phangan?

Yes, Koh Phangan is only a 30-minute ferry ride from Koh Samui. Our island visit package includes round-trip ferry tickets and private hotel transfers for an easy full-day self-paced excursion.
