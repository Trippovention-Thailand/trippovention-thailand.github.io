Tour Overview & Description

Experience Northern Thailand's most breathtaking architectural and spiritual landmarks

Discover the cultural and natural highlights of Chiang Rai on this full-day guided excursion. Relax at the famous Mae Kachan Hot Springs before visiting the stunning Blue Temple (Wat Rong Suea Ten) and the world-renowned White Temple (Wat Rong Khun), celebrated globally for their remarkable contemporary Buddhist architecture and intricate artwork.

Enjoy a delicious authentic Northern Thai lunch, unwind in the picturesque fairy-tale surroundings of Lalitta Café, and visit the serene Wat Huai Pha Kiang. This tour seamlessly blends spirituality, art, natural landscapes, and local culture, making it an essential journey for travelers wanting to discover the best of Chiang Rai.

Places Covered

Iconic temples, thermal hot springs, and picturesque stops included in your day

Masterpiece Architecture

Famous Temples & Shrines

  • Wat Rong Khun (White Temple): Master artist Chalermchai Kositpipat's visionary masterpiece of pure white plaster and shimmering mirrored glass.
  • Wat Rong Suea Ten (Blue Temple): A dazzling modern sanctuary distinguished by vivid ultramarine hues and intricate gold leaf ornamentation.
  • Wat Huai Pha Kiang: A tranquil, spiritually enriching temple set in serene Northern Thai mountain greenery.
Nature & Lifestyle

Hot Springs & Scenic Cafés

  • Mae Kachan Hot Springs: Natural thermal mineral springs where you can soak your feet and observe boiling geyser pools.
  • Lalitta Café: A fairy-tale garden café with mystical mist, tropical waterfalls, vibrant blooms, and unique photo spots.
  • Northern Countryside: Scenic highway views through mountain passes connecting Chiang Mai and Chiang Rai.
