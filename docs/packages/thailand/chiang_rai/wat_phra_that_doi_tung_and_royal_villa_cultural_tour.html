Tour Overview & Description

Experience the royal heritage, sacred architecture, and mountain elegance of Doi Tung

Discover the cultural and royal heritage of Chiang Rai on this half-day guided tour to two of the province's most revered and iconic landmarks. Visit the sacred Wat Phra That Doi Tung, one of Northern Thailand's oldest and most venerated temples, perched majestically atop Doi Tung Mountain with sweeping panoramic views of the Thai-Myanmar borderlands.

Continue to the elegant Doi Tung Royal Villa, the former mountain residence of the beloved Princess Mother (Srinagarindra), celebrated for its harmonious blend of Swiss chalet and traditional Lanna architecture. Stroll through the lush alpine floral displays and discover the Princess Mother's royal development initiatives that transformed the region.

Places Covered

Historic royal sites and sacred mountaintop attractions visited on this excursion

Sacred Landmark

Wat Phra That Doi Tung

  • Ancient Twin Stupas: Sacred Buddhist pilgrimage site dating back over a thousand years, enshrining holy relics.
  • Mountain Summit Panorama: Breathtaking views stretching across mist-covered peaks towards neighboring Myanmar.
  • Spiritual Atmosphere: Experience the soothing sound of temple chimes and gentle mountain breezes.
Royal Heritage

Doi Tung Royal Villa & Gardens

  • Royal Villa Architecture: Built in a charming Swiss-Lanna architectural fusion using sustainable recycled teakwood.
  • Princess Mother's Legacy: Learn how royal agricultural and reforestation projects rejuvenated local tribal lives.
  • Mae Fah Luang Gardens: Manicured temperate flower beds featuring hydrangeas, orchids, and seasonal alpine flora.
