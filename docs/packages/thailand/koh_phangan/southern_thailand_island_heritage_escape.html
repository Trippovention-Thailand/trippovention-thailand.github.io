Southern Thailand Island & Heritage Escape

Discover the perfect combination of tropical islands and authentic Thai culture as you explore Koh Phangan, Koh Samui, and Surat Thani with pristine beaches, scenic viewpoints, waterfalls, temples, and local experiences.

🗓️

Duration

Detailed Itinerary

Day 1

Arrival in Koh Phangan

  • Arrive at Koh Samui Airport.
  • Meet & greet by our local representative.
  • Transfer to the ferry pier and board the ferry to Koh Phangan.
  • Upon arrival, transfer to your hotel.
  • Check in and relax after your journey.
  • Spend the remainder of the day at leisure.
Meals: None
Day 2

Koh Phangan Nature Discovery

  • Breakfast at the hotel.
  • Visit the scenic Bottle Beach Viewpoint.
  • Relax at the secluded Bottle Beach.
  • Explore Than Sadet Waterfall National Park.
  • Visit Haad Rin Beach, one of Koh Phangan's most famous beaches.
  • Return to the hotel in the evening.
Meals: Breakfast
Tickets: Included
Day 3

Transfer to Koh Samui & Temple Discovery

  • Breakfast at the hotel.
  • Check out from the hotel.
  • Transfer to the ferry pier and board the ferry to Koh Samui.
  • Visit the iconic Big Buddha Temple (Wat Phra Yai) (Complimentary Stop).
  • Visit Wat Plai Laem (Complimentary Stop).
  • Transfer to your hotel and check in.
  • Spend the evening at leisure.
Meals: Breakfast
Day 4

Koh Samui Sightseeing Tour

  • Breakfast at the hotel.
  • Proceed for a Koh Samui Highlights Tour.
  • Visit Na Muang Waterfall.
  • Explore the famous Grandfather & Grandmother Rocks (Hin Ta & Hin Yai).
  • Stop at Lad Koh Viewpoint.
  • Relax at Silver Beach.
  • Return to the hotel.
Meals: Breakfast
Tickets: Included
Day 5

Transfer to Surat Thani

  • Breakfast at the hotel.
  • Check out from the hotel.
  • Transfer to the ferry pier and board the ferry to Surat Thani.
  • Visit the beautiful Surat Thani City Pillar Shrine (Complimentary Stop).
  • Explore the lively Surat Thani Night Market (Complimentary Stop – Evening, subject to operating days).
  • Check in to your hotel.
Meals: Breakfast
Day 6

Surat Thani Cultural Experience

  • Breakfast at the hotel.
  • Visit the magnificent Wat Phra Borommathat Chaiya Worawihan, one of Southern Thailand's most important historic temples.
  • Explore Chaiya National Museum.
  • Enjoy a scenic walk at Bueng Khun Thale Lake.
  • Return to the hotel in the evening.
Meals: Breakfast
Tickets: Included
Day 7

Departure

  • Breakfast at the hotel.
  • Check out from the hotel.
  • Transfer to Surat Thani International Airport for your onward journey.
  • Depart with unforgettable memories of Southern Thailand.
Meals: Breakfast
