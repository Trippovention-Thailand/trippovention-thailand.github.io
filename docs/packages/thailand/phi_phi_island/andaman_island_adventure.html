Andaman Island Adventure

Discover the perfect blend of island charm, coastal adventures, and vibrant nightlife as you explore Phi Phi Island, Krabi, and Phuket. From breathtaking viewpoints and island-hopping to world-class entertainment, this itinerary offers a unique experience without repeating attractions.

Detailed Itinerary

Day 1

Arrival & Transfer to Phi Phi Island

  • Arrive at Phuket International Airport.
  • Meet & greet by our local representative.
  • Transfer to Rassada Pier.
  • Board the ferry to Phi Phi Island.
  • Check in to your hotel.
  • Spend the evening exploring Ton Sai Village.
  • Relax at Loh Dalum Beach and enjoy the island nightlife.
Meals: None
Day 2

Phi Phi Island to Krabi

  • Breakfast at the hotel.
  • Visit the famous Phi Phi Viewpoint for panoramic island views.
  • Check out and board the ferry to Krabi.
  • Transfer to your hotel and check in.
  • In the evening, visit the Ao Nang Landmark Night Market (Complimentary – subject to operating days).
Meals: Breakfast
Day 3

Krabi 7 Islands Sunset Tour

  • Breakfast at the hotel.
  • Enjoy the popular 7 Islands Sunset Tour.
  • Visit Chicken Island.
  • Relax at Tup Island.
  • Explore Poda Island.
  • Snorkel around Koh Tan Ming.
  • Enjoy a BBQ dinner on the beach while watching the sunset.
  • Experience bioluminescent plankton swimming (weather permitting).
  • Return to the hotel.
Meals: Breakfast, BBQ Dinner
Tickets: Included
Day 4

Transfer to Phuket

  • Breakfast at the hotel.
  • Check out and drive to Phuket.
  • En route, enjoy a photo stop at the Sarasin Bridge Viewpoint (Complimentary).
  • Arrive in Phuket and check in.
  • Evening free to explore Patong Beach.
Meals: Breakfast
Day 5

Old Phuket Town & Cultural Experience

  • Breakfast at the hotel.
  • Explore the colorful streets of Old Phuket Town.
  • Visit Karon Viewpoint.
  • In the evening, enjoy the spectacular Carnival Magic Phuket show.
Meals: Breakfast
Tickets: Included
Day 6

Phuket Leisure & Adventure

  • Breakfast at the hotel.
  • Spend an exciting day at Andamanda Phuket Water Park.
  • Evening free for shopping or relaxing.
Meals: Breakfast
Tickets: Included
Day 7

Departure

  • Breakfast at the hotel.
  • Check out from the hotel.
  • Transfer to Phuket International Airport.
  • Depart with unforgettable memories of Southern Thailand.
Meals: Breakfast
