Andaman Island Serenity Escape

Detailed Itinerary

Day 1

Arrival & Transfer to Phi Phi Island

  • Arrive at Phuket International Airport.
  • Transfer to Rassada Pier.
  • Board the ferry to Phi Phi Island.
  • Check in to your hotel.
  • Spend the evening exploring Ton Sai Village and the nearby beachfront.
  • Overnight stay in Phi Phi Island.
Meals: None
Day 2

Phi Phi Island to Phuket

  • Breakfast at the hotel.
  • Visit the famous Phi Phi Viewpoint for spectacular panoramic views.
  • Check out and board the ferry back to Phuket.
  • Upon arrival, transfer to your hotel and check in.
  • In the evening, enjoy Siam Niramit Phuket, showcasing Thailand's rich culture through a world-class theatrical performance.
  • Overnight stay in Phuket.
Meals: Breakfast
Tickets: Included
Day 3

Phuket Adventure

  • Breakfast at the hotel.
  • Spend the day at Andamanda Phuket Water Park.
  • Evening free to relax or explore nearby cafés and shopping areas.
Meals: Breakfast
Tickets: Included
Day 4

Transfer to Koh Yao Yai

  • Breakfast at the hotel.
  • Check out and transfer to the pier.
  • Speedboat transfer to Koh Yao Yai.
  • Check in to your beachfront resort.
  • Relax at Laem Had Beach, one of the island's most scenic beaches.
  • Overnight stay in Koh Yao Yai.
Meals: Breakfast
Day 5

Koh Yao Yai Nature Experience

  • Breakfast at the hotel.
  • Visit Ao Muong Beach.
  • Explore the peaceful Loh Pared Beach.
  • Visit a local rubber plantation to learn about traditional rubber tapping.
  • Enjoy the island's serene atmosphere before returning to the resort.
Meals: Breakfast
Tickets: Included
Day 6

Departure

  • Breakfast at the hotel.
  • Check out from the resort.
  • Speedboat transfer back to Phuket.
  • Transfer to Phuket International Airport for your onward journey.
Meals: Breakfast
