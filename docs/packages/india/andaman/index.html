Frequently Asked Questions

Essential travel guide for your Andaman tropical island holiday

What is the best time to visit the Andaman Islands?

The best time to visit Andaman is between October and May, when the tropical weather is sunny, rainfall is minimal, and underwater visibility is perfect for scuba diving, snorkeling, and sea walking at Havelock and Neil Island.

Which islands and attractions are covered in Andaman packages?

Our itineraries feature Port Blair (Historic Cellular Jail Light & Sound Show, Corbyn's Cove), Havelock Island (Asia's famous Radhanagar Beach No. 7, Elephant Beach coral reef), and Neil Island (Natural Rock Bridge, Bharatpur Beach, Laxmanpur sunset point).

How do travellers from Thailand visit Andaman?

Travellers fly from Bangkok to Kolkata or Chennai (approx. 2.5 hours), followed by a connecting domestic flight directly to Port Blair Veer Savarkar International Airport (IXZ). Trippovention coordinates all land ferries, inter-island catamarans, and hotel stays.

Are water sports and scuba diving suitable for beginners?

Yes! Elephant Beach and Nemo Reef offer introductory scuba diving and helmet sea walking supervised by PADI-certified dive masters. Non-swimmers and first-time adventurers can safely explore the coral reefs.
