Attraction Name: Krabi Hot Springs & Emerald Pool (SIC Transfer)

Price PP: THB 950

Description:
Escape into the natural beauty of Krabi with a relaxing visit to the famous Hot Springs and the stunning Emerald Pool. Begin your journey at the natural hot springs, where you can unwind in warm mineral-rich pools surrounded by lush rainforest. Continue to the Emerald Pool, a crystal-clear natural lagoon renowned for its vibrant turquoise waters, perfect for a refreshing swim. Surrounded by tropical greenery and peaceful walking trails, this tour offers the perfect combination of relaxation and nature, making it an ideal experience for families, couples, and nature lovers.

Places Covered:

* Krabi Hot Springs
* Emerald Pool Nature Reserve
* Rainforest Walking Trail
