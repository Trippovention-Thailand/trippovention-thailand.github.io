Attraction Name: Krabi 7 Islands Sunset Tour by Speedboat (SIC Transfer)

Price PP: THB 1,300

Description:
Experience one of Krabi's most popular island-hopping adventures as you explore seven stunning islands by speedboat. Cruise through the crystal-clear waters of the Andaman Sea, stopping at picturesque islands and secluded beaches for swimming, snorkeling, and relaxation. Visit iconic destinations including Chicken Island, Tup Island, Poda Island, and the famous Phra Nang Cave Beach. As evening approaches, witness a breathtaking sunset over the Andaman Sea before enjoying a delicious beachside dinner. End the day with an unforgettable opportunity to swim among glowing bioluminescent plankton (seasonal and weather permitting), creating a truly magical experience.

Places Covered:

* Poda Island
* Chicken Island
* Tup Island
* Phra Nang Cave Beach
* Railay Beach
* Yawasam Island
* Koh Tan Ming Island
