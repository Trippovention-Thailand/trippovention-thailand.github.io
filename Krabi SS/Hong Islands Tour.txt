Attraction Name: Hong Islands Tour by Speedboat (SIC Transfer)

Price PP: THB 1,270

Description:
Discover the breathtaking beauty of the Hong Islands on an exciting speedboat adventure through the crystal-clear waters of the Andaman Sea. Explore pristine white-sand beaches, hidden lagoons, and dramatic limestone cliffs while enjoying opportunities to swim, snorkel, and relax amidst unspoiled natural surroundings. Visit the iconic Hong Lagoon, known for its emerald-green waters enclosed by towering cliffs, and spend time soaking in the tranquil atmosphere of Hong Island Beach. A delicious lunch box is included, making this tour an ideal choice for nature lovers, families, couples, and adventure seekers looking to experience one of Krabi's most picturesque island destinations.

Places Covered:

* Hong Island
* Hong Lagoon
* Lading Island (Paradise Island)
* Pakbia Island
