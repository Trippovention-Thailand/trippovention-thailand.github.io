Attraction Name: Elephant Sanctuary – Morning Program (SIC Transfer)

Price PP: THB 1,560

Description:
Spend a memorable morning at an ethical elephant sanctuary, where you'll interact closely with rescued elephants in their natural habitat. Begin with a welcome drink and an introduction to traditional rubber tree cultivation before walking through the jungle alongside these gentle giants. Feed the elephants, enjoy a fun mud spa, help bathe them, and freshen them up with a shower. The experience also includes a hands-on Pad Thai cooking session, offering a perfect blend of wildlife, nature, and Thai culture for visitors of all ages.
