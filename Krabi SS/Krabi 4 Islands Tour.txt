Attraction Name: Krabi 4 Islands Tour by Speedboat (SIC Transfer)

Price PP: THB 930

Description:
Embark on an exciting island-hopping adventure to four of Krabi's most breathtaking islands by speedboat. Cruise across the crystal-clear waters of the Andaman Sea and discover the stunning beauty of Poda Island, Chicken Island, Tup Island, and the famous Phra Nang Cave Beach. Relax on pristine white-sand beaches, swim in turquoise waters, snorkel among vibrant marine life, and witness the unique sandbar connecting Tup Island during low tide. Enjoy a delicious lunch box while surrounded by spectacular tropical scenery, making this tour an ideal choice for beach lovers, families, couples, and adventure seekers.

Places Covered:

* Poda Island
* Chicken Island
* Tup Island
* Phra Nang Cave Beach
